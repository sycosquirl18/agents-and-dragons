## Condition

**Unharmed.** No named injuries.

## Skills

- **Glass-framing and lead-setting** — cutting, fitting, and sealing panels; reading how a piece has been joined.
- **Forging documents** — commission papers, licences, Concord transit stamps.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — Works quickly and quietly. Leaves a room cleaner than she found it, except when she doesn't.
- **Bond** — Mabb Orrin, fence in Catch. She owes him two separate debts and has not paid either.
- **Flaw** — Takes things that are not hers when she is confident she will not be caught. She is wrong more often than she knows.

## Background

Born in Catch to a family of stilt-house repairers. Apprenticed at thirteen to a glass-house in the lower market. Made her name framing cinder-glass panels for Vaultspire buyers who could not transport them unframed. Did good work for six years.

In 412-191 she finished a three-panel commission for a [Covenant](../../world/factions/ashen-covenant.md) intermediary and walked out with the smallest panel under her coat. Twenty-three days of silence from the buyer. On day twenty-four, Catch woke up with two Covenant retrieval agents asking after her at the market. She has not left the upper walkways since.
