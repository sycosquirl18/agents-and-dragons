# Ren Tallow — Record

412-214 — Left the Ashen Covenant's Cant chapterhouse in Vaultspire after flagging a pre-Turn document that
contradicted the official account. Arrived at [Catch](../../world/geography/sites/catch.md) with a pack, a knife,
and fourteen years of notation training nobody in the Saltmarch has any use for yet.

412-215 — Walked onto Catch's market floor and found two Ashen Covenant retrieval agents four paces off, between
her and the ladders. Approach 1d20 [19] — she saw them first. They had been hunting
[Tessaly Orr](../tessaly-orr/sheet.md) since Day 214 and gave it up the moment Ren arrived. No writ, no rope: an
offer, not a taking. [What the Covenant Buys](../../quests/what-the-covenant-buys.md).

412-215 — Rather than run, Ren announced on Catch's market floor that the Covenant had bought access to the Widow
Sarn's ladders. Heart DC 16: 1d20+3 [10] + 3 = 13, failed. The floor went silent rather than hostile; a rack-hand
near the Sarn ladders reacted and did not look up. Ren is now publicly known to know, and cannot be bought quietly.
The agents closed the last two paces.
