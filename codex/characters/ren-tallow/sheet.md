## Skills

- **Kilnworks notation** — years copying pre-Turn fragments for the [Ashen Covenant](../../world/factions/ashen-covenant.md)'s case file. She reads it faster than she reads Vaultspire trade-cant.
- **Argument** — trained to make a weak claim sound load-bearing in front of a chapterhouse tribunal. Works less well on people who don't care about precedent.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — footnotes everything out loud, mid-sentence, whether or not anyone asked.
- **Bond** — Catechist Ambrey Doss, her old teacher in the Cant, senior enough in the Covenant to protect her and not yet told she's gone.
- **Flaw** — cannot let a contradiction stand unflagged, even when flagging it is the thing that gets her noticed.

## Background

Fourteen years cataloguing pre-Turn fragments for the Covenant's case against the debt the Kiln-lords supposedly
owe. Found a document that didn't fit the [Hundred-Year Turn](../../world/history/events/the-hundred-year-turn.md)
story the Covenant tells about itself, and said so, out loud, in the wrong room. Walked out of the Cant chapterhouse
before anyone decided what to do about that. Still writes — pamphlets, testimonials, whatever pays — because it's
the only thing she knows how to do, and because the notation on the walls of the
[Drowned Kiln](../../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) reads like the fragment that started this.
