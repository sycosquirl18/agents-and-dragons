# Ren Tallow — Journal

## 412-214

Catch smells like low tide and it is not yet noon and I have already been asked twice whether I'm Covenant. I say no
both times and both times it's a lie of omission, which Ambrey always said was the worst kind because it costs you
nothing to fix and you don't.

I brought the fragment. I shouldn't have — it isn't mine, not really, it's the Cant's, or it's the Kiln-lords', or
it's nobody's, which was rather the point I tried to make in that room. But the marks on it are the marks people
keep saying are turning up again near the Kiln, and I would like, once, to be first to a document instead of
fourteenth in line behind four hundred years of people telling me what it already says.

Somewhere out past the Saltmarch the water's gone warm enough to worry salvors, which takes some doing. I intend to
find out why before the Covenant does, and before I let myself think too hard about what happens if I'm wrong about
all of it a second time.
