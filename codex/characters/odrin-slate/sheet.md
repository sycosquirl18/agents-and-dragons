# Odrin Slate

Scrap-shearer, runs a shear-shed on the Kiln-Road edge of Vaultspire, cutting condemned salvage nobody wants a
licence to touch.

## Condition

**Unharmed.** No named injuries. See the [harm ladder](../../rules/combat.md#harm).

## Skills

- **Shearing and scrapping** — reading condemned cinder-glass and Kilnworks metal for what can still be cut, sold,
  or is about to kill him. −3 DC.
- **Hauling** — carrying, dragging, and rigging loads no sane single man should move alone. −3 DC.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — Talks to the scrap while he cuts it, like it can hear him deciding whether it's worth saving.
- **Bond** — His sister still dives the Underspire he pulled himself out of years ago; he has never once asked her
  to stop.
- **Flaw** — Cannot leave a piece of cinder-glass scrap unclaimed, even when it is plainly someone else's find. It
  has made him enemies among crews who found it first.

## Background

Grew up diving the [Underspire](../../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md#districts) alongside his sister until
his lungs gave out younger than most. Took up shearing instead — cutting down what condemned salvage crews bring up
too broken or too hot to sell whole. Works alone at the edge of town where the [Kiln
Road](../../world/geography/sites/the-kiln-road.md) meets Vaultspire, close enough to buy from wagons before they
reach the Cant's licensed yards.
