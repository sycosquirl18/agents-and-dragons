# Corwin Ash — Journal

## 412-214

Scrubbed a hull-plank clean this morning and found a bootprint pressed into the rot underneath it — not mine, not
old. Told Marra. She just looked at the water and said the tide's early again, four minutes, same as always, and
that I should worry about my own four minutes before I worry about hers.

Fair. But I'm still going to ask around about the bootprint.
