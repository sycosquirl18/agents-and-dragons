# Corwin Ash

Wrack-scrubber at [Catch](../../world/geography/sites/catch.md), paid to sterilize salvage of rot and worse before
the Concord will license it. Reads tide by ear, same as everyone else arguing about the four minutes.

## Stats

Rolled `4d6` per stat, no re-rolls, no swaps.

| Grace | 18 | +4 | `4d6 [6, 6, 5, 1] = 18` (natural 6 lead die) |
| Wits | 13 | +1 | `4d6 [6, 2, 1, 4] = 13` (natural 6 lead die) |
| Heart | 11 | +0 | `4d6 [3, 1, 2, 5] = 11` |

## Condition

**Unharmed.**

## Skills

- **Wrack-sterilizing** — burning, salting, and picking-over drowned salvage for rot, taint, and things that came
  up alive.
- **Tide-reading** — counting the water by feel and by ear, no bell required.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — Narrates his own work under his breath, like he's reporting to someone who isn't there.
- **Bond** — [Marra Whit](../../world/people/marra-whit.md), whose reading of the early tide he trusts over the
  bell and has said so, loudly, to people who matter.
- **Flaw** — Cannot leave a piece of wrack half-cleaned. Will go back for the rest of a job after the tide has
  already turned.

## Background

Learned the sterilizing trade off a Concord contractor who left Catch owing him three weeks' wages and never came
back. Corwin kept the trade, not the debt, and has been scrubbing drowned salvage clean ever since — harassed
weekly by the same contractor's creditors, who are convinced he knows where the man went.
