# Gault Marrow

Gambler out of the Ashfields, currently unwelcome in three granges and owed money by a fourth.

## Stats

Rolled `4d6` per stat, no re-rolls, no swaps.

| Stat | Score | Mod | Tape |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Might | 17 | +3 | `4d6 [2, 4, 6, 5] = 17` |
| Grace | 22 | +6 | `4d6 [6, 6, 6, 4] = 22` (natural 6 lead die) |
| Wits | 7 | −2 | `4d6 [4, 1, 1, 1] = 7` |
| Heart | 16 | +3 | `4d6 [3, 3, 6, 4] = 16` |

Total of modifiers is +10 — no adjustment applies.

## Condition

**Unharmed.**

## Skills

- **Card and dice sharping** — reading a table, palming, marked decks, knowing when a game is rigged against him.
- **Wagon driving** — tight turns, bad roads, spooked teams.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — Never raises his voice, even when winning.
- **Bond** — The Long Room at [Threshing](../../world/geography/sites/threshing.md), which he cannot walk into
  without an armed escort and does anyway.
- **Flaw** — Cannot leave a rigged game unplayed. He knows the house always sets the table; he sits down regardless.

## Background

Won a wagon and its team off a Concord buyer at Threshing two seasons back — square game, the buyer just couldn't
count. The buyer complained to the wrong grange. Gault kept the wagon, lost the welcome, and has been circling the
Ashfields since, half daring someone to make him give it back.
