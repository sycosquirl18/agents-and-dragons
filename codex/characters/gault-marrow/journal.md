# Journal — Gault Marrow

**Day 214.** Camped off the Kiln Road, wagon wheels still caked in Threshing mud. I could sell the team and be
three granges richer in welcome. I won't. A man who gives back a fair win teaches everyone at the table that
losing loud enough gets your money back, and I've seen where that road ends — it ends with everybody at the table
being right and nobody playing again.

Elm Cassardy hasn't sent anyone yet. That's either patience or a longer memory than mine. Either way, I'd rather
find out which sitting at a table than running from one.
