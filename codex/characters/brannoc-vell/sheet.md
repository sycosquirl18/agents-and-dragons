# Brannoc Vell

Salvage-diver, forty-one, of [Tideline](../../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md). Licence revoked spring 412 for
failing to report a find. He did report it. The clerk who lost the paperwork was
[Harrow Mecks](../../world/people/harrow-mecks.md), who has been apologetic and useless about it since.

## Condition

**Wounded** — *right hand torn* (two fingers stripped on the slot rim). Disadvantage on anything needing a firm
grip or fine work with that hand. See the [harm ladder](../../rules/combat.md#harm). It will not close; he worked
the diver's ribs left-handed rather than test it.

**Marked** — a long cut down the left side, ribs to hip, taken blind at the sill. The oiled leathers turned what
should have been a second Wounded; they are cut through and need repair before they will do it again.

**No air.** He put [the Unspooled Line](../../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) in Ress Adair's mouth instead of
his own. He has since taken one mouthful of the Kiln's exhaust at the lip — foul, searing, and real — and spent it
on the chain. There is no third blackout in him.

**Shallows: 10** — recruited 412-214. Not yet spent: the fumble at the sill on 412-215 was a roll meant to kill
him, and the shallows took the DC down to nothing rather than the once-only clause firing. It is the only reason
there is a sheet to update.

## Skills

- **Diving** — depth, tides, lines, the specific arithmetic of held breath. −3 DC.
- **Kilnworks salvage** — knowing what a thing was for, and what it is worth. −3 DC.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — Answers questions with the smallest true thing that will end the conversation.
- **Bond** — Old Pellick taught him to dive and is now dying badly in a Tideline room Brannoc pays for.
- **Flaw** — He will not leave a body down there. Not anyone's. It has nearly killed him twice and he does not
  consider this an argument.

## Background

Twenty-two years licensed. He has been deeper in the Underspire than anyone alive except Pellick, and unlike Pellick
he is still going. Since the revocation he has been diving anyway and selling to a fence at half price, which is
slowly ruining him and which he has not mentioned to Pellick.

Three crews have gone into the [Drowned Kiln](../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md) and not come up. One of them
was crewed by people he trained.
