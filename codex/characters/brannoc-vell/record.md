# Brannoc Vell — Record


One line per thing that happened. Newest at the bottom. This is the ledger the
[Dungeon Master](../../../.github/workflows/dungeon-master.md) reads to judge pace and pressure without paying for
the whole [journal](journal.md).

- **412-214** Went down through the outer sluice into [the Drowned Kiln](../../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) without a licence.
- **412-214** Worked the fouled lock on the inner grate and got it open. Grace 17.
- **412-214** Found one of Sella's crew dead inside the inner grate — no wound, no explanation. Went deeper on held breath and rope; reached the lip of a gallery below the passage floor. Amber glow inside. Second unmoving diver visible on the gallery floor. Rope ran out; turned for surface. Wits+Diving [13]+2=15.
- **412-214** Surfaced. Four hours to tide. Umber Cord chain on the beach — long enough but fouled at three pins. Chain serviceability 1d20 [7] — not ready.
- **412-214** Attempted to crack fouled shackle pins on Umber Cord chain; knife blade snapped on calcified lock-wire. Chain still locked at all three pins. Knife blade lost. Wits+Kilnworks salvage [1]+2=3 — fumble.
- **412-214** Pellick produced an iron drift and a ballast stone. Pellick's answer 1d20 [16] — a real one. Three pins still to break, under four hours of tide.
- **412-214** Broke all three Umber Cord shackle pins with Pellick's drift and a ballast stone — refused to take the chain short, on the grounds he cannot carry a body up a gap. Might [11]+1=12, [11]+1=12, [10]+1=11. Chain free, forty feet. Roughly three hours of tide left.
- **412-214** Lashed the living second diver to himself and tried to haul her up the leaning chain on a spent breath. Came off the iron twelve feet up. Might+Diving [2]+1=3. Right hand torn on the slot rim (Wounded). Pellick's drift lost into the gallery. Both of them now in the passage above the slot, sixty feet down, breath gone.
- **412-214** Did not drown. The passage roof held a hand's depth of trapped air, hot and lime-tasting, seeping up from the gallery. Air 1d20 [13] — present, foul. Breathing it; so is the unconscious diver. Two hours of tide, sixty feet of black water above, and no way up that does not go back to the chain.
- **412-214** Spent the second breath trying to bring the diver round in the air pocket rather than starting the climb. Wits+Diving [5]+2=7, disadvantage (torn hand) — she breathes on her own but weakly, still unconscious. Air pocket measurably smaller for the effort.
- **412-214** Lashed Ress Adair to his chest and climbed the now-plumb *Umber Cord* chain one-handed, salvage hook turned over the torn wrist. Might+Diving [2]+1=3, disadvantage — off the iron at twenty feet, kept hold of her, lost the salvage hook into the slot. Both back in the air pocket, which no longer refills.
- **412-215** Air left in the pocket 1d20 [5] — a finger's depth, one attempt. Someone came up through the slot: hooded, breathing without surfacing for it, carrying a coil that is not rope. Disposition 1d20 [15] — not there to stop him. It offered the coil and pointed down, toward the ember. Ninety minutes of tide.
- **412-215** Took the stranger's coil and went **down** through the slot on the last of the pocket, Ress Adair still lashed to his chest — refused to leave her, and refused to tie the stranger's coil to her. Grace+Diving [6]+2=8, disadvantage — through, but uncontrolled; a turn of the chest harness torn out on the rim. Both on the gallery floor with an empty chest and no pocket to return to. The stranger followed them down.
- **412-215** On the gallery floor with nothing left in him, the hooded stranger put [the Unspooled Line](../../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) to his mouth and it breathed for him. Stranger's action 1d20 [11] — it acted, and not against him. Ember cooled 1d20 [4] — dim enough to touch, and the wall behind it is not a wall. Sixty minutes of tide. The Line supplies one, and the stranger holds the other end.
- **412-215** Took [the Unspooled Line](../../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) out of his own mouth and put it in Ress Adair's — refused to be the one breathing while she was not. Heart, DC 13 (−2, apt): [9]−1=8 — she breathes; he does not. The far end stayed in the stranger's hand and there is no slack for two. Empty chest on the gallery floor, sixty minutes of tide, cooled ember, open wall.
- **412-215** The stranger stepped over him and put both hands to the wall behind the cooled ember; the wall withdrew. Stranger's action 1d20 [8] — it did not help him, it went on with its own errand. Hot scorched-lime air came out of the opening hard enough to lift silt. It walked in, paying [the Unspooled Line](../../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) out behind it, Ress Adair still breathing off the near end. Consciousness on an emptied chest 1d20 [12] — still awake, not for long. Fifty-five minutes of tide.
