# Old Pellick

Former licensed salvor. Claimed he would not go back to the Drowned Kiln after year 408; is currently in
the [settling galleries](../../world/geography/sites/the-settling-galleries.md). Knows the inner grate in ways
he has not explained. The kiln-wights remember him.
