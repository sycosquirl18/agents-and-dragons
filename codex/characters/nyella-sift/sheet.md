# Nyella Sift

Uncertified clerk who forges Concord paperwork out of a back room in Vaultspire — licences, manifests, burial-society
receipts, whatever a scoundrel with the right coin needs to look legitimate.

## Condition

**Unharmed.** No named injuries. See the [harm ladder](../../rules/combat.md#harm).

## Skills

- **Forging Concord paperwork** — licences, manifests, register entries; close enough to pass any clerk who isn't
  looking for it. −3 DC.
- **Talking her way past a locked door** — bluffing guards, clerks, and enforcers into believing she belongs
  wherever she's standing. −3 DC.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — Signs every forgery with a tiny sifted mark only she would notice, like she's daring someone to catch
  her.
- **Bond** — Owes her start to **[Harrow Mecks](../../world/people/harrow-mecks.md)**, the licence-clerk who taught her the register's blind spots and
  now expects a cut of everything she sells.
- **Flaw** — Cannot resist improving on a forgery past what the job needed, leaving a trail of paperwork too good
  to be real. It has nearly gotten her caught twice.

## Background

Grew up sorting drowned salvors' estates for the Concord's burial-society ledgers, where she learned exactly how
little anyone checks a stamped page. Never held a licence herself — never wanted one, given what she's watched the
Concord do to people who have. Works out of a back room near the licensing offices, selling paper to whoever the
Concord's [scarcity management](../../world/factions/salvors-concord.md#wants) has locked out.
