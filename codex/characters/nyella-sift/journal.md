# Nyella Sift — Journal

## 412-214

Harrow wants his cut again. I told him the register doesn't check itself, and neither does his conscience, but
I paid him anyway — cheaper than a Concord audit.

Three revoked licences came through the back room this week, all divers who'd rather forge a name than beg the
Concord for a favour they can't afford. I gave one of them a manifest so clean I nearly kept it for myself. That's
the trouble with doing this well — nobody remembers to thank you, and everybody remembers the mark.

Someone's buying salvage rights around the Drowned Kiln at prices that don't make sense. When money stops making
sense, someone always needs a clerk who doesn't ask why.
