# Perrin Fen — Journal

## 412-214

Coss says leave it, the Concord will send someone. Coss is seventy-one and hasn't been below Tideline in a decade.
Every lamp in the rendering-house has been burning blue at the root for a week, and blue at the root means the
draft's wrong, and the draft's wrong means something's pulling air it shouldn't. I know fire. I don't know what's
doing this to it. Going down to the waterline to find out, because somebody who actually looks at flames for a
living ought to be looking at this one — the whole vault's furnace, still burning, and the water over it running
warmer than it's ever run.
