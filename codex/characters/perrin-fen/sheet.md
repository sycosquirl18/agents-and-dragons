# Perrin Fen

Might 15 (+2) · Grace 18 (+4) · Wits 12 (+1) · Heart 16 (+3)

Rolls (4d6 drop lowest): Might `[2,1,6,6]`→15 · Grace `[6,3,6,3]`→18 · Wits `[4,4,3,1]`→12 · Heart `[2,4,5,5]`→16.

**Condition:** Unharmed.

## Skills

- **Burner and lamp repair** — every rendering-vat and tideline stove in Vaultspire that still lights, he's touched.
- **Working in a crawlspace** — Underspire ducting, vault crawlways, anywhere a grown man shouldn't fit but does.

## Trait, bond, flaw

- **Trait** — unnervingly calm with his hands an inch from open flame.
- **Bond** — his old master, Coss Ambry, who taught him the trade and is too old now to climb to the vats himself.
- **Flaw** — cannot leave a wrong-burning flame alone, even when it isn't his to touch, even when someone tells him not to.

## Background

Grew up under Coss Ambry rendering tallow and fish-oil for Tideline's lamp trade — work that lives or dies on a
flame behaving exactly as it should. Lately the burners have stopped behaving: flames running too hot, too blue,
guttering for no draft anyone can find. Word from the Underspire is the water over the Drowned Kiln is doing the
same thing. Perrin came down to the waterline to find out why, on his own time, because the fire is wrong and
nobody who isn't him seems bothered enough to fix it.
