# The Chronicle

What actually happened, in the order it happened. **Append only.** Nothing here is ever edited or deleted — if the
record is wrong, the world is wrong, and that is now a fact about the world.

| Entry | |
| --- | --- |
| [412-214 — The Seal Was Cut](412-214-the-seal-was-cut.md) | Brannoc finds the Kiln's outer grate opened from within |

Entries are named `<year>-<day>-<slug>.md`. The [Chronicler](../../.github/workflows/chronicler.md) folds them into
[era summaries](../world/history/README.md) as they age; the raw entries stay where they are, forever.

## Writing an entry

Short. A few paragraphs at most, in the world's voice, past tense. It records **what happened and what changed** —
not what it means, and not what anyone plans to do about it. Interpretation is the historians' job and they are
allowed to get it wrong.

Link the people, places and quests involved. Do not restate their details.
