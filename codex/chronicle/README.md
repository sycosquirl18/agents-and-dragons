## Writing an entry

Short. A few paragraphs at most, in the world's voice, past tense. It records **what happened and what changed** —
not what it means, and not what anyone plans to do about it. Interpretation is the historians' job and they are
allowed to get it wrong.

Link the people, places and quests involved. Do not restate their details.
