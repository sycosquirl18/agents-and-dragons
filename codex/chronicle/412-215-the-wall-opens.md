# 412-215 — The Wall Opens

With [Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) empty on the gallery floor and Ress Adair breathing on
[the Unspooled Line](../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md), the hooded stranger did not kneel again. It gathered the
far end of the Line, stepped over him, and went to the cooled ember.

It put both hands flat on the wall behind the ember, low down, and the wall went back. Not a door swinging — a face
of fired clay withdrawing into itself, the way a held breath goes out.

What came through the opening was air. Hot, scorched-lime, moving fast enough to lift silt off the gallery floor and
push it out through the slot. The same air that had filled the roof pocket for two days, arriving all at once, from
whatever is on the other side.

The stranger walked into the opening. The Line paid out behind it, still in Ress Adair's mouth, and it did not stop
paying out.

Fifty-five minutes of tide. See [The Kiln Still Burns](../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md).
