# 412-214 — The Chain Is Fouled

[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) came up through the sluice at mid-morning of Day 214 and
found the tidal gauge on the outer wall reading worse than it had at dawn. The neap window was shrinking faster
than the moon's schedule said it should. Four hours, not six.

[Old Pellick](../world/people/old-pellick.md), waiting on the shingle with Brannoc's spare kit, had an answer to
the rope problem: a length of chain from the wreck of the *Umber Cord*, pulled up on the last salvage season and
never sold, lying coiled under an oilskin fifty yards up the beach. Long enough to reach the gallery floor. Heavy
enough to stay taut in any current.

The chain was coral-fouled and salt-locked at three of its shackle pins. Usable in a week with proper tools, or
in two hours with a hammer and a prayer, or not at all if the tide moved faster than expected.

*Chain serviceability (1d20 [7]): fouled — workable but not ready.*

Brannoc had four hours and a problem that was not yet solved.
