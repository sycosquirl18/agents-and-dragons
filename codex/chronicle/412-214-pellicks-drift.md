# 412-214 — Pellick's Drift

Old Pellick did not answer the question about the pins. He walked up the shingle to where his boat sat on its side,
put his arm in under the thwart, and came back with a drift — a tapered iron punch as long as a forearm, its head
mushroomed flat by thirty years of being hit. Kilnworks iron, off the *Umber Cord* before she went. He said the pins
were not rusted in, they were grown in, and grown things break clean if you hit them once and mean it.

He gave Brannoc the drift and a fist-sized ballast stone to swing, and then he sat down on the shingle facing the
water, which he had not done all morning, and said the tide would not wait for three pins.

Below, in the gallery under the passage floor, the second diver's chest moved. Once, slowly, in water that should
have drowned them two days ago. The amber deepened behind them.
