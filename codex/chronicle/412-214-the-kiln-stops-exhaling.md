# 412-214 — The Kiln Stops Exhaling

The seep failed. Not thinned — stopped. The slow warm breath coming up through the slot into
[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md)'s pocket quit between one breath and the next, and the water in
the slot went flat and still with it.

Seep behaviour, 1d20 [2] = 2 — failure. Whether anything in the gallery marked the pause, 1d20 [4] = 4 — nothing did.

What was left in the roof of the passage was what two people had not yet breathed, and no more was coming. But the
Kiln paid the debt in a currency he had not asked for: below him the amber light in the gallery dimmed down to a
sullen ember, and the pull that had been leaning the *Umber Cord* chain off plumb slackened. For the first time
since he broke the pins, the chain hung true.

The water in the slot began to cool. Not cold — cooler, and dropping, the way a shed cools when the doors are shut.

Roughly two hours of tide, sixty feet of black water, an unconscious diver who cannot climb, and a chain that has
stopped fighting him. [Quest](../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md).
