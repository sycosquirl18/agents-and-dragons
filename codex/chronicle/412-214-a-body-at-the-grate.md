# 412-214 — A Body at the Grate

[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) passed the inner grate of [the Drowned Kiln](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) and found the first of the lost.

One of Sella's crew, still in harness, wedged against the passage wall as if the current had pressed them there. No wound. No tool in hand, no signal line cut, no mark of struggle. The diver had simply stopped, the way a candle stops when the air runs out — except nothing here had taken the air.

The water past the grate was hotter than in the sluice. From somewhere below, a faint light rose: amber, slow, not the colour of a lamp.

Brannoc's rope still ran back to the surface. He had two minutes of breath remaining.

*Roll — What Brannoc finds (1d6): [2] = a body, no clue. 1d20 [7] vs DC 12 — the body tells nothing.*
