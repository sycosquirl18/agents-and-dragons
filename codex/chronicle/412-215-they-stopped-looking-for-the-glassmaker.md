# 412-215 — They Stopped Looking for the Glassmaker

For a day and a half the two men from the [Ashen Covenant](../world/factions/ashen-covenant.md) had worked
[Catch](../world/geography/sites/catch.md) the way retrieval is done: the racks, the fence, the ladder-wardens,
every lane of the lower town at ebb, and [Tessaly Orr](../characters/tessaly-orr/sheet.md) still fifteen feet
above their heads and not given up.

On the morning of the fifteenth they stopped. They did not leave and they did not go up. They took a position on
the market floor with the ladders behind them and waited, and what they were waiting for walked in off the
Vaultspire road with a pack on her back.

[Ren Tallow](../characters/ren-tallow/sheet.md) saw them at four paces. Approach 1d20 [19]. They carried no writ
and no rope, which in that trade means an offer rather than a taking, and it meant something else besides: they
knew what she was carrying, and they wanted the paper more than they wanted the woman they had come for.

Somewhere above, a ladder that belongs to the Widow Sarn had already been paid for by somebody. Ninety minutes to
the Turn, which Catch rings four minutes early and has all year.
