# 412-215 — Someone Comes Up the Slot

Air remaining in the roof pocket: 1d20 [5] = 5 — a finger's depth, and the taste of scorched lime gone sharp.

In the dark above [the slot](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md#the-gallery-below-the-passage),
[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) lay with Ress Adair still lashed to his chest and worked out
that the pocket had one more attempt in it and not two.

Then the water in the slot broke.

Something came up through it that was a person: hooded, unhurried, breathing without surfacing for it, carrying a
coil of line that was not rope. It put a hand on the passage roof, found the last of the air with the certainty of
someone who had known it was there, and did not use it. Disposition 1d20 [15] — it did not come to stop him.

It looked at Ress Adair a long moment. Then it held out the coil, and pointed down — back through the slot, toward
the ember — not up.

The tide had ninety minutes left in it.
