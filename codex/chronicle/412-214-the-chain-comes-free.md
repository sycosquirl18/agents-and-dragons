# 412-214 — The Chain Comes Free

On the shingle below [the Drowned Kiln](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md),
[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) broke the three grown-in shackle pins of the *Umber Cord* chain
with the iron drift Old Pellick had given him, one swing to a pin, a ballast stone for a hammer. All three went out
clean.

He had been offered the shorter road and did not take it: one pin broken would have freed enough chain to hang above
the gallery floor and cost him far less of the tide. He spent the tide instead, on the stated grounds that a chain
ending above the floor is a chain he cannot carry anyone up.

Forty feet of chain lay coiled and free on the beach with roughly three hours of neap water left in the window.
Pellick did not go near the waterline. See [The Kiln Still Burns](../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md).
