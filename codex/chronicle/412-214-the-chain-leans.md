# 412-214 — The Chain Leans

[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) took the freed *Umber Cord* chain back down into
[the Drowned Kiln](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) and fed it through the slot into the gallery
below the passage floor. Sella's second diver lay where she had lain, face down on the gallery floor, still
breathing — slowly, and on nothing anyone could name.

The chain did not hang straight. Forty feet of Kilnworks iron stood off plumb in still water, leaning steadily
toward the amber light deeper in the gallery, and the lean grew heavier the longer he held it. The water was
warmer that way.

He had one lungful and one chain, and the two things they could do were not the same thing.
