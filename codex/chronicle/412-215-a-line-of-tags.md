# A Line of Tags

Day 215. Six feet downstream of the halted wall, [Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) fanned the
silt off the wrist in his fist and found it would never let go, because it was not buried. The shoulder of
[Hob Cardew](../world/people/hob-cardew.md) is set into the floor of
[the Drowned Kiln](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md)'s gallery, cured into the clay like a bracket
into a wall. Beyond him the bared floor shows three more tags in a line, a pace apart, at the same depth —
laid out, not fallen.

Above, the tide turned early, as the Saltmarch has been turning all year. The jet at the wrist-wide gap began to
slacken: the making water is coming down to meet the Kiln's breath, and where they meet there will be no breath at
all. Ress Adair goes on breathing off
[the Unspooled Line](../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md), held to a man by one turn of rope.

Four names, then. Not three crews lost in the dark — four men fitted into a floor, in order.
