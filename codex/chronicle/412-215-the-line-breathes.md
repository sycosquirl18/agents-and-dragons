# 412-215 — The Line Breathes

On the floor of [the gallery below the passage](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md#the-gallery-below-the-passage),
with his chest empty and no air above him, [Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) began to go out.

The hooded figure that had followed him down through the slot knelt, took the coil from his wrist, and put the end
of it to his mouth. [The Unspooled Line](../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) opened its strands and breathed into
him — hot, tasting of scorched lime, the same air the roof pocket had held. He took it. He is still taking it.

The figure did not offer the other end to Ress Adair, who lay lashed to him and breathing on her own. It has the
coil in one hand. It has not moved to give it up, and it has not moved to take it back.

The ember at the far wall has cooled to something a hand could hold. Behind it, where the glow used to be too
bright to look at, the wall is not a wall. Sixty minutes of tide.
