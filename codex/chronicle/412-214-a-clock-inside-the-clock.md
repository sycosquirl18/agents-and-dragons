# 412-214 — A Clock Inside the Clock

In the passage above the slot, [Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) spent his returned breath on the
woman he had carried up rather than on the sixty feet of water over them both. He got her face into the pocket and
worked her ribs with his left forearm, the right hand being torn open and useless for it.

Resuscitation, Wits + Diving (DC 12), disadvantage: 1d20+2 [5] + 2 = 7.

She did not wake. She coughed water, weakly, and went on breathing on her own, which is a different thing and he
knew it. She cannot climb. Whatever gets her out of [the Drowned Kiln](../world/geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md)
will have to carry her.

The pocket paid for it. Two of them breathing drew the trapped air down by about a finger's depth in the time the
work took, and the seep from the gallery below refills it at the rate the Kiln exhales and not at the rate anyone
needs. The scorched-lime taste came up stronger as it thinned.

Roughly two hours of tide. The *Umber Cord* chain still hangs below, still leaning toward the glow.
