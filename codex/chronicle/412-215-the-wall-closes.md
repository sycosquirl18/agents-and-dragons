# 412-215 — The Wall Closes

[Brannoc Vell](../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) came to at the lip with nothing in him, and found that the
Kiln was still breathing out. The air off the opening reached the lip — not clean, not enough to live on for
long, but there, close enough that a face turned into it got something.

He did not have to go through to breathe. He had to hold his mouth in the exhaust.

Then the clay began to come back. The face that had withdrawn into itself was easing out again, without haste and
without sound, the way it had gone. The gap was still wide enough for a man with a body strapped to him. It was
narrower than it had been.

Through it, a body's length of what lay beyond: floor that looked dry, a light that was amber and steady rather
than dying, and [the Unspooled Line](../world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) running taut away across it and still
paying out. No stranger. No ceiling. No way to tell whether the far side held water at all.

Fifty minutes of tide. See [The Kiln Still Burns](../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md).
