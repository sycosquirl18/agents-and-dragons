# Knotroot Clam

Fist-sized bivalve that roots along the chain and rope work of
[the Drowned Kiln's](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) outer sluice, thickest where the amber heat runs
strongest. It closes on anything that brushes it, including its neighbours, and grows a knot of shells fused
shoulder to shoulder wherever the current is slow enough to let one hold on. A dense knot underwater sounds like a
short, dull thud when it seals.

Licensed crews eat them raw off the chain on the way down — six hours in cold water goes better on a full stomach,
and a knotroot cracked open with a knife is the only hot food anyone gets at that depth. Nobody sells them; there is
no way to bring a live knot up faster than it spoils.

```
Stats:      Might +0  Grace -3  Wits -4  Heart -2
Wants:      to stay shut, stay attached, and filter warm water
Presses:    a disturbed knot seals every shell within reach at once, pinning fingers or rope in the chain; a badly
            timed harvest can strip enough of a knot to weaken the chain's grip on the silt bed it anchors; a
            crushed shell vents a stinking cloud that draws glass-crakes
Beaten by:  a slow hand and a dull knife — Grace DC 8 to work one free without triggering the seal-reflex on the
            rest of the knot
Shrugs off: force, speed, and anything that isn't patient
```

Crews read the size and colour of a knot as a rough gauge of how strong the heat is running that season — thicker,
paler knots near the inner grate this year than anyone remembers charting before.
