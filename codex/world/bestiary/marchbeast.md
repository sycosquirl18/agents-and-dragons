# Marchbeast

A lean, antlered thing the height of a draft horse, found on [the Kiln Road](../geography/sites/the-kiln-road.md)
between Threshing and the Spine. Each one has claimed a single stretch of paving — never more than a hundred
strides square — and walks its bounds in dead-straight lines and exact right angles, day and night, without
resting.

It does not chase wagons and does not follow the road past its own claim. Crews who keep moving pass through
unbothered, sometimes without noticing it at all. Crews who stop, camp, or make repairs inside its square are
another matter: the marchbeast will not attack outright, but it paces closer with every hour they linger, tightening
its lines until it is walking a circle around the camp instead of a square around its ground.

Nobody has found two marchbeasts on the same stretch, and nobody has found one off the paving. Whatever keeps it
to its exact acreage, it treats the flagstones themselves as the boundary — one hoof off the Road's edge and it
turns back like it hit a wall that isn't there.

Stats:      Might +2  Grace +1  Wits −1  Heart +0

Wants:      Pace its claimed square of the Road, undisturbed, forever.

Presses:    First press: a camp set up inside its ground gets a warning lap, close enough that its hoofbeats are
            audible over conversation. Second press, if the camp is still there an hour on, it starts walking a
            tightening spiral inward, forcing the party to break camp or fight in cramped, shrinking ground of its
            choosing. Third press, if cornered or struck, it stops pacing entirely and comes straight at whoever
            hit it — the only time it moves off a straight line.

Beaten by:  Moving on before the second press costs nothing — it never follows past its square's edge. Wits DC 13
            to pace out its exact boundary from the first lap's sound and camp just outside it. If a fight starts,
            Might DC 15 to withstand its first straight-line charge; it does not press a second.

Shrugs off: Fire, noise, and thrown missiles from outside its square — it keeps its lines and does not react to
            anything that isn't inside its ground.
