# Toll-creeper

*[Catch](../geography/sites/catch.md), lower town, at ebb.*

A knuckle-sized shore crab that never moves alone. Where the tide leaves lower Catch dry, toll-creepers pour up
out of the stone lanes' drain-cracks in the hundreds, going straight for anything salted, tallowed, or roped —
drying racks first, rope ladders second. Nobody owns them and nobody can stop them arriving; the only argument is
who clears them off first, and that argument is why half of lower Catch smells of woodsmoke two hours before every
Turn.

Stats:      Might +1  Grace +3  Wits +0  Heart +1
Wants:      Strip drying racks and rope of anything edible or greasy before the Widow's crews can clear it.
Presses:    Arrives by the hundred, not the one — a cleared rack draws a second wave within minutes; a swarm
            gnaws through a rope ladder's lashings in one ebb if left, costing the Widow a route; driven off a
            rack, it moves to the nearest rope or foot instead, so clearing one thing exposes another.
Beaten by:  Smoke or a low fire on the stone (DC 12, Wits or Grace) scatters a swarm for the rest of the ebb;
            racks slung above the last year's ebb-line are never reached at all.
Shrugs off: Killing individuals does nothing — treading on a dozen barely thins a swarm in the hundreds; salt
            water does not deter them, they came up out of it.
