# Ash-thrum

A finger-length burrowing cricket that lives in the two feet of fertile ash across the
[Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) and nowhere else. Granges read its chorus, not the sky, to decide
when to plant, when to dig storage pits, and when to move stock off low ground.

Stats:      Might +0  Grace +2  Wits +1  Heart +0
Wants:      to burrow, chorus at dusk, and go quiet before the ash itself changes — damp, frost, or something worse
Presses:    a grange ignores a stopped chorus and plants anyway; the ash-thrum goes silent for days at a time this
            season with no frost to explain it; a full field falls silent at once, which has never happened before
            record began
Beaten by:  not a fight — read correctly (Wits DC 12) it gives a full day's warning of any change in the ash;
            caught for grange rafters, a jar chorus dies out within a week and tells nothing more
Shrugs off: killing them for silence — a dead thrum answers nothing; salting the ash to drive them out, which they
            simply out-burrow

The three granges that have stopped selling this season are the same three whose fields have gone silent early.
Nobody has said this out loud yet.
