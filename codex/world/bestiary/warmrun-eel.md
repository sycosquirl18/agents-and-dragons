# Warmrun Eel

A slack-tide migrant, the length of a forearm and the width of two fingers, that runs the Underspire's flooded
galleries in a single dense shoal whenever the water below is warmer than the water above. Nobody in
[Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) used to notice them; the shoals were thin and passed in minutes.
This year the run has been thick enough to see from a lantern's edge, and it no longer stops when the tide turns.

The run is tied to the same warming the Concord is not explaining — see
[Vaultspire's dangers](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md#what-is-hidden). Nonviolent exit: go cold and dark, not loud.

```
Stats:      Might +1  Grace +3  Wits -2  Heart -1
Wants:      to reach warmer water. Follows heat through the nearest gap, gallery, or diver.
Presses:    the shoal compresses through any opening a hand fits through, fouling lines and hookholds;
            a diver's own lantern or breath-warmth draws it in close enough to mar bare skin with rasping mouths;
            enough of them in a passage blind you and drown out every other sound with the flurry of bodies
Beaten by:  killing the light — Grace DC 14 to shutter a lantern or hold breath still until the shoal loses the
            scent and moves on
Shrugs off: blades and grabbing; cutting or catching a few does nothing to the shoal's direction
```

Not aggressive — a Warmrun Eel has no interest in a diver except as a patch of warmth to sit against. That is worse
than hostile in a flooded gallery: it will not be driven off by pain, only by going cold and dark. Old salvors say a
thick run means the water below is heating early. This year the run started before neap tide.
