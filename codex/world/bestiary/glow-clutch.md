# Glow-clutch

A soft mass of finger-length grubs that clings to warm pipe-joints in the
[Underspire](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md), each one carrying a bead of
cold light in its tail. Salvors farm them: scrape a clutch onto a hook and it
lights a gallery for a watch, feeding on the film of waste warmth and scale
that beads off old ironwork. Left alone they breed slow and glow steady.

The trouble is what they answer to. A glow-clutch does not track light or
food — it tracks *heat*, and it will abandon a hung lamp-hook mid-watch for
any warmer thing that reaches it, the water above the kilns this year being
warmer than any lamp a crew can carry.

Stats:      Might -2  Grace +1  Wits -1  Heart +1

Wants:      to cluster on the warmest surface within reach and glow there, undisturbed

Presses:
- **Drifts toward true heat.** A held lamp loses its clutch to any stronger
  warmth nearby — the kilns, a lit forge, a fevered hand — darkening the
  gallery a crew thought was lit.
- **Blisters when startled.** Struck, scraped roughly, or doused, the clutch's
  beads burst in a rush, guttering out in a fog of sour steam that stings the
  eyes and kills nearby flame — the clutch goes dark defending itself.
- **Regroups on the nearest warm body.** A burst clutch reclusters within
  minutes, on whatever is warmest close by, crew included, in a cold gallery.

Beaten by:  patience — leaving a clutch a cooling scrap of scale to feed on
holds it in place (DC 12, Wits); moving it hook to hook without jostling
keeps it lit (DC 14, Grace)

Shrugs off: killing it outright — a crushed clutch blisters exactly as a
startled one does, dousing the light and choking the passage regardless
