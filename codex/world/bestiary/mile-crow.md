# Mile-crow

Ivory-pale crows that ride the rails of wagons on [the Kiln Road](../geography/sites/the-kiln-road.md), thickest past
Threshing where traffic thins. They do not follow a wagon out of hunger — they follow it for the voices, and give
them back wrong.

A mile-crow flock strings together scraps of every voice it has ever ridden beside: a toll demand, a haggling
price, a prayer, a name called out once and never again. Crews four days east of Threshing use the mix as a rough
gauge of how far they've come — familiar Vaultspire cant thinning out, stranger accents layered in, until the birds
are cawing words in no language a crew recognises at all.

Stats:      Might −1  Grace +3  Wits +1  Heart −1
Wants:      Ride the wagon, listen, and strip anything left unweighted or unlashed.
Presses:    First press takes small unsecured cargo — a coin purse, a loose strap-end, a forgotten glove — while
            the flock caws back the last thing you said, word for word, a beat late. Second press, disturbed or
            shooed, the whole flock lifts at once and screams every voice it has been carrying over each other,
            spooking draft animals and drowning out anyone trying to be heard. If the wagon stops moving for long,
            a third press: they start on the lashings themselves, working a knot loose one beak-pull at a time.
Beaten by:  Weighting or lashing cargo down before the eastern stretch avoids the theft outright. Grace DC 12 to
            catch a bird mid-snatch and get the item back; Wits DC 13 to pick a true warning or message out of the
            mimicry once the flock has heard it.
Shrugs off: Chasing them off — they simply resettle on the next wagon-length back. Loud noise only triggers the
            second press early.
