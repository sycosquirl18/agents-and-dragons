# Gasp-Limpet

A glassy, thumbnail-sized shell that colonises the undersides of trapped air pockets in the Underspire's flooded
galleries — the same kind of pocket a stranded diver breathes from. It does not hunt divers; it hunts the air
itself, filtering the gas at the water-line and thickening its shell a grain at a time. A pocket left alone for a
season is furred solid with them.

Old salvors call a heavily-clung ceiling "bearded," and read it as a pocket that has been sealed a long time — which
means the air in it has been breathed thin by the colony long before any diver gets to it.

```
Stats:      Might +0  Grace +1  Wits -2  Heart -2
Wants:      to keep filtering the gas at the pocket's ceiling. Does not react to divers unless the pocket itself
            is disturbed.
Presses:    a thriving colony has already thinned the pocket's air, cutting a diver's safe time there sooner than
            the pocket's size would suggest; scraping or striking the ceiling showers loose shells and grit into
            the water, foul­ing lanterns and grip; a big enough disturbance breaks the colony's seal on the water-line
            all at once, venting the pocket's remaining air out through the gallery in a single rush
Beaten by:  scraping a colony off before breathing there — Might DC 15 with a blade or tool, done slow and away
            from the water-line so the seal doesn't break early
Shrugs off: killing a few shells does nothing; the colony is the pocket's ceiling, not a single creature, and it
            has no fight in it to lose
```

Nonviolent exit: leave the ceiling alone. A Gasp-Limpet colony never presses on its own — every danger here comes
from disturbing it, not from being near it. See [The Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) for where
this is worst.
