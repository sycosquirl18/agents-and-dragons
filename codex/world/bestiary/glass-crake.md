# Glass-crake

Long-legged wading bird of the [Glass Wound](../geography/regions/the-glass-wound.md) that swallows chips of
cinder-glass and does not pass them. A crake's crop is therefore worth roughly a month's wages, which the crakes have
begun to notice.

They nest high, they remember faces, and they are not remotely afraid of people.
