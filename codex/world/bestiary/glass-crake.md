# Glass-crake

Long-legged wading bird of the [Glass Wound](../geography/regions/the-glass-wound.md) that swallows chips of
cinder-glass and does not pass them. A crake's crop is therefore worth roughly a month's wages, which the crakes have
begun to notice.

They nest high, they remember faces, and they are not remotely afraid of people.

```
Stats:      Might -1  Grace +4  Wits +1  Heart +0
Wants:      to swallow every chip in sight, and to be somewhere you are not
Presses:    goes for the eyes; screams, and the whole flock answers; takes the glass and is airborne with it
Beaten by:  patience and bait — Wits DC 13 to set a lure, no roll at all if you simply let it eat and follow it home
Shrugs off: threats, ambush, and being chased on foot
```

Killing one is trivial and ruins the crop. Everyone in [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) has an
opinion about the right way to take a crake, and all of them are wrong in a different way.
