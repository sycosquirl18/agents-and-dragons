# Sizzlewax Barnacle

Clusters low on [Catch's](../geography/sites/catch.md) pilings and ladder-anchors, below the flood line. At ebb
it is a dull grey crust; at flood, submerged, each shell opens a soft siphon and jets a hot, glassy resin over
anything that lingers against it — driftwood, rope, a boot sole — to armour the colony against tide scour. The
resin sets in seconds and cures harder than the wood or rope it coats. Vaultspire glaziers buy the cured shards
as a cheap red-brown pigment; nobody has told the Widow Sarn's ladder crews that the colony is why some rungs
near the waterline never wear smooth.

Stats:      Might +1  Grace +0  Wits -2  Heart -2
Wants:      To armour itself — jet resin on whatever touches the colony and hold still until it cures
Presses:
  - First contact: jets scalding resin at the offending limb or object — Grace DC 12 to flinch clear, or take a
    scorch (Marked).
  - Ignored or trapped against the colony: the resin skins over in one exchange and starts to grip — freeing a
    coated limb, rope, or rung before it fully sets is Might DC 15.
  - Left three exchanges or more: the layered colony can seal a rung or ladder strand solid, cured glass-hard —
    the only ways through are cutting the rope elsewhere, going around, or waiting for the next scour to crack
    it loose.
Beaten by:  Not lingering against a submerged piling at flood — Wits DC 14 to recognise the crust and the sizzle
            of a working colony before it triggers. A tossed offering of driftwood or scrap redirects one jet's
            worth of resin away from a person, DC 12.
Shrugs off: Cutting or burning cured resin — it is glass, not wood, and holds an edge better than most knives
            keep one. Pulling straight back out of half-set resin only speeds the cure.
