# Sluice-grebe

A hand-sized diving bird that nests inside Vaultspire's overflow channels and drainage weirs. During nesting season the drains back up and Tideline flooding runs twenty to thirty minutes past schedule — enough to trap the unprepared.

The bird locates nest sites by following warm air drawn out of the vault's interior through the overflow pipes. It treats any channel with a steady warm exhaust as sheltered. It does not attack. It does not flee easily. It simply nests.

Late autumn is nesting season. The current neap tide runs the kilns hotter than usual, which means the exhaust is stronger than usual, which means there are more grebes than anyone remembers seeing in a season.

```
Stats:    Might -2  Grace +3  Wits +0  Heart +1
Wants:    incubate eggs in a dark, warm pipe above the waterline; dive for fish at low tide
Presses:
  - On disturbance: abandons the nest for twenty minutes, then returns and rebuilds faster
  - On nest removal: relocates to the next nearest warm channel within the hour; three more birds follow
  - On flood-tide: dives and stays under 4–5 minutes; during this window the channel is clear
Beaten by: removing nest while bird is submerged (Grace, DC 12 to reach inside the pipe in time); or sealing
  the exhaust opening to kill the warmth draw (Wits, DC 14 to identify the right pipe)
Shrugs off: poles, torches, loud noise — habituated to the city
```

Its nests are woven reed cups sealed with mucus and cinder-dust, inverted inside the pipe so the cup faces down over the water. The eggs are slate-grey and practically invisible. Tideline residents sell them in the market; they taste of brine and old iron and are considered a delicacy by people who have eaten worse.
