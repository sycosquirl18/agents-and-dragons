# Settle-crow

A black, oil-slicked scavenger of the [Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md), never seen except at a
grange gate the moment a grain deal is struck — spilled husks, a slaughtered ox, the mess a bargain leaves behind.
Ashfielders read a gathering of settle-crows the way sailors read a falling glass: not the trouble itself, but the
sign one is due.

They do not scavenge open ground. They wait on a wall or a rafter, restrained and silent, until coin and grain
actually change hands — then they drop as one and clean the ground in minutes. A grange with no crows at its gate
has not settled anything in a while, and everyone in earshot knows it.

```
Stats:      Might +0  Grace +2  Wits +2  Heart -1
Wants:      to feed on what a completed bargain leaves behind — and to be first to it
Presses:    a flock rises screaming the instant a deal is called off, as if cheated; mobs anyone carrying grain who
            hasn't yet been weighed in, on the assumption the transaction is already done; roosts thicker on granges
            about to fail, driving off buyers who know the sign
Beaten by:  finishing what you started — Wits DC 12 to strike a visible bargain (a spoken price, a counted sack)
            calms a mobbing flock at once, since the crows only ever wanted the settlement over
Shrugs off: being fed, being scattered by noise, being killed — the flock simply thins for an afternoon
```

Three granges have stopped selling to [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) this season and started
storing instead — their settle-crows have not been seen in weeks, and the granges that still deal are starting to
notice the silence over the wall as much as the birds themselves.
