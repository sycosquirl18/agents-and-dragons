# Marrowbriar

A root-mass under the [Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md), threaded through the ash itself. Grange
farmers know it by the bramble it sends up between planting and harvest — thorny, fast-growing, always cut back and
always returning thicker where the soil is best. Nobody admits to knowing more than that.

It is the reason the ash is still fertile after four hundred years. It is fed, every season, in a grange rite none
of the granges will describe to an outsider or to each other. What it is fed, and by whom, is not written down.

A grange that skips the rite gets one bad harvest as a warning. A grange that skips it twice loses the field —
not to failure, but to bramble that will not be cut, cannot be burned, and takes the topsoil down to bare stone
within a year.

```
Stats:      Might +3  Grace +1  Wits -2  Heart +2
Wants:      to be fed on schedule; to spread wherever a field goes untended
Presses:    thorn-wall closes off a retreat; roots buckle the ground underfoot; a fed patch nearby answers by
            surging toward whoever is bleeding
Beaten by:  the grange rite, correctly performed — Heart DC 16 to bargain a season's reprieve without paying it;
            burning a patch back takes days of fire-tending and only holds one season
Shrugs off: a single fire, a single cutting, being dug out — it returns from any root left in the ash
```

Killing it in one field is possible. Killing it everywhere would end four hundred years of Ashfield grain, which
is why no grange has ever tried, and why the six documents they keep from before year 100 are rumoured to say what
it actually is.
