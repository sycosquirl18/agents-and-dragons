# Weepbloom

A fungal mat that grows in the warm damp of the [Weeping Bore's](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md) idle
faces, feeding on lamp tallow and the fine glass-dust cutting leaves behind. It has no single body — a bloom is a
sheet of pale threads with one swollen node buried at its centre, the only part that can die and take the rest
with it.

Left alone it thickens quietly. Fed — spilled lamp oil, a dropped ration, an unswept cutting floor — it swells
fast enough to close a gallery in a night, and its threads work into cinder-glass seams before anyone notices,
souring the grain so the [Concord's](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) assayers disqualify the whole face from
Plate grade on sight. A bloom does not care whether a level is on the rota. It has been thriving on Level 6 for as
long as it has sat idle.

```
Stats:    Might -3  Grace -3  Wits -3  Heart +2
Wants:    spread through damp, unlit cracks; consume tallow, oil, and glass-dust; be left alone
Presses:
  - Fed (spilled fuel, dropped food, careless waste): swells by a third overnight, threading toward the nearest
    cut face
  - Threaded into a seam: the glass souring becomes visible within a day; the face is disqualified from Plate
    grade at next assay
  - Disturbed at the node (cut, burned, dug at): releases a sour, choking spore-cloud in the enclosed gallery —
    Might DC 12 to get clear before a coughing fit costs the exchange
Beaten by: finding and destroying the buried node (Wits DC 14 to trace the threads to their thickest point,
  then Might DC 12 to dig it out or burn it clean); starving it by sealing off fuel and light for a full tide
Shrugs off: scraping the surface threads, cold water, poking at the mat anywhere but the node
```

It is not hunted or eaten — cutters call disturbing one "feeding the Bore twice" and avoid it. A bloom big enough
to have a name is worth more to a Concord assayer paid to certify a face clean than any glass pulled from it.
