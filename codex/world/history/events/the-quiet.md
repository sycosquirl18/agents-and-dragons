# The Quiet

*Year 380, from before dawn until mid-evening. Eleven hours.*

Every kiln stopped.

Not banked, not damped — **stopped**, in every vault in the known world, at what witnesses in places a hundred miles
apart independently describe as the same moment. The [Drowned Kiln](../../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md) under
[Vaultspire](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) went cold enough that the Underspire's flooded galleries
dropped to sea temperature for the first time in recorded history.

Eleven hours later they all lit again. Together.

## What was seen in the gap

Salvors went down. Almost nobody had ever been into a cold kiln, and for eleven hours anybody could be.

The accounts agree on the following and on almost nothing else:

- The kiln chambers are **not damaged**, not sooted, and show no fuel of any kind.
- The interior surfaces carry [notation](../eras/the-kilnworks.md#the-notation-problem), densely, everywhere — and
  it is the only notation in the world found in a place that was never meant to be entered.
- Four separate crews report the notation **had changed** when they came back with copyists during hour nine. Their
  first sketches and second sketches do not match. All four sets survive; the discrepancies are real; no scholar has
  produced an account of them that anyone else accepts.

## Afterwards

- Nineteen people died when the kilns relit while they were inside. The
  [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) paid the families and classified the surveys.
- Kiln chambers have been off-licence ever since — not forbidden, which would invite argument, merely never granted.
- Salvage yields began their decline the following decade. Whether this is connected is the most respectable of the
  unrespectable questions.

## The thing nobody says out loud

Eleven hours is a long time for a fault and a short time for a decision. Both readings assume the kilns are the sort
of thing that can have either, and the [Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) has been very careful never to
say which it believes.
