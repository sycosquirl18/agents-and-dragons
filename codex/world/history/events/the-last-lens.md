# The Last Lens

*Year 408. Four years ago, and still the most expensive four minutes in Vaultspire's history.*

A **Lens** is Kilnworks-made [cinder-glass](../../../rules/magic.md): flawless, castable at DC 11, and unmakeable.
Eleven were known to survive. Ten sat in institutional hands — Concord vaults, the Covenant chapterhouse, two
sealed bequests nobody expects to open.

The eleventh belonged to the Faidh family, who had held it for six generations without ever spending it, on the
grounds that a thing which cannot be replaced is not really wealth until you need it.

In 408 they needed it. They sold.

## The sale

The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) bought it at auction for a sum the Concord has never
published, against bidders who dropped out one at a time and have all since declined to say why.

The Lens went into the chapterhouse in the Cant and has not been seen since. It has not been **spent** — casting a
Lens is public by nature and nobody has reported one — so somewhere in Vaultspire there is a piece of glass worth
more than the district it sits in, doing nothing.

## Why this is the hinge of the era

Every Lens now sits behind an institution. That means:

- **Private magic in this world is finished.** What remains in circulation is Chips and Plates, which are salvage
  grade, and the supply of those is falling too.
- **Whoever holds Lens glass holds the only remaining reliable magic**, and there are four institutions and eleven
  pieces.
- The Covenant began buying **dead salvors' rights** the same year — see
  [Vaultspire](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md). Nobody has explained the coincidence of dates and the
  Covenant has not been asked in public.

## Open

- What the Covenant paid, and to whom the money went. The Faidh debts it cleared were not large enough.
- Why the underbidders withdrew.
- What a Lens is *for*. Everyone treats them as very good Plates. The Kilnworks made them, and the Kilnworks did not
  make redundant things.
