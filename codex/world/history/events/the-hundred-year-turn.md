# The Hundred-Year Turn

*Circa year 100. Not a day, and not an event anyone noticed happening.*

Between roughly year 90 and year 115, every surviving account of the [Sundering](../eras/the-sundering.md) changes
its explanation, and they all change it the same way.

| Written before ~100 | Written after ~100 |
| --- | --- |
| Something went wrong at the kilns | The Kiln-lords did it deliberately |
| Sudden, localised, unforeseen | Long-planned, world-spanning, deserved |
| The Kiln-lords are missing | The Kiln-lords fled what they had done |
| Grief | Judgement |

Nobody announced this. No council ruled on it. There is no first document that argues the new position — the new
position simply *is* the position, in every text after a certain point, stated as though it had never been
otherwise.

## Why it matters

The turn is the foundation of the moral order this world still lives in. If the Kilnworks destroyed themselves
through ambition, then this age's refusal to build anything new is **wisdom**. If it was an accident, then four
centuries of scavenging has been cowardice with a good excuse.

The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) was founded on the orthodox reading and everything it owns
depends on it. The [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) is officially indifferent and privately grateful,
since an age that fears invention is an age that keeps paying for salvage.

## The uncomfortable part

The turn happens **exactly as the last people who could have remembered year 0 die.**

That timing is either the ordinary way memory becomes myth, or it is the point. Scholars who raise it are not
persecuted; they are simply not published, which the [Reaving](../eras/the-reaving.md) taught everyone is
cheaper.

## Open

- No pre-Turn document has ever been found in Vaultspire itself. Six exist in the
  [Ashfields](../../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md), which nobody has satisfactorily explained.
- The Covenant's chapterhouse library is closed to outsiders and is known to hold at least two.
