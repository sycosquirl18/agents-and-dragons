# The Glass Peace

*Year 212, [Vaultspire](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md). Signed in eleven days, enforced for two hundred.*

Four licence houses had spent a century underbidding each other into ruin. In 212 they stopped competing and merged
into the [Salvor's Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md), which from that day forward was the only body in
Vaultspire permitted to grant the right to dive.

It is called a peace because the alternative on the table was another [Reaving](../eras/the-reaving.md), and
everybody involved had grandparents who remembered the first one.

## What was actually agreed

| Clause | Effect then | Effect now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One licence authority | Ended the bidding war | The Concord decides who may become rich |
| Salvage must be **reported** before sale | Stopped houses hiding finds from each other | Unreported salvage is the commonest crime in the city |
| Unlicensed diving forfeits **air** | A brutal deterrent everyone expected to lapse | Never repealed; still enforced by the enforcers |
| Licences are **revocable at will**, no cause required | Nobody argued; the houses trusted each other | The single greatest power in Vaultspire |

That last clause was an afterthought written to save arguing about procedure. It is now the entire structure of
Vaultspire's politics.

## The part the Concord does not display

The Peace was signed by four houses. **Five had been invited.** The fifth, the Ossary remnant, refused, and its
holdings in the [Underspire](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) were declared unlicensed within the year and
taken.

There are galleries down there that the Concord's charts do not show, and Old Pellick's forty-year-old hand-drawn
chart does. Nobody has connected these two facts in public.

## Consequences

- Vaultspire tripled in size over the following century.
- Nowhere else in the known world adopted the licence system, and the
  [Ashfields](../../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) consider it a racket in writing.
- The Concord dates itself from **year 83** rather than 212 whenever the difference between an agreement among
  equals and a monopoly comes up.
