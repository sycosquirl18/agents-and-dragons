# The Sundering

*Year 0. One day, possibly less.*

The western coast went under and did not come back. The [Glass Wound](../../geography/regions/the-glass-wound.md)
opened inland along a line no river or fault accounts for. Ash fell east for a season, and the
[Ashfields](../../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) have been the best farmland in the world ever since.

Every Kiln-lord was gone by the end of it. Not killed — there are no bodies anywhere, in a catastrophe that left
bodies everywhere else. Their servants, guards, and families were left behind and had no account of it either.

## Contested

Accounts written after year 100 blame the Kilnworks for causing it deliberately. Accounts written *before* year 100 do
not, and read like people describing an industrial accident. Which of these is propaganda is an open question and the
[Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) has a large stake in the answer.

## What it left

- The Wound, and therefore [cinder-glass](../../../rules/magic.md), and therefore magic.
- A drowned coast that is walkable at low tide.
- The kilns, still burning. Nobody has explained this in four hundred years.
