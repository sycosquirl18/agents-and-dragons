# The Long Salvage

*Year 83 to the present. Three hundred and twenty-nine years of living inside a better age's leftovers.*

The [Reaving](the-reaving.md) ended when taking vaults by force stopped paying. What replaced it was not peace
exactly — it was **the licence**: the legal right to bring something up, granted by whoever controls the hole.

Nothing has been invented since that the Kilnworks did not already build better. The culture has largely stopped
trying, and does not experience this as a loss.

## What the era believes

- **That the wealth is behind the next door.** Four centuries of sealed vaults have not dented this.
- **That salvage is a trade, not a harvest.** Trades continue. Harvests end.
- **That the Kilnworks were destroyed by their own ambition**, and therefore that this age's lack of ambition is
  prudence rather than exhaustion. This belief is exactly as old as the Hundred-Year Turn.

## What is actually happening

The salvage is running out. It has been running out for thirty years, measurably, and the
[Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md)'s response has been to issue fewer licences rather than admit why —
which looks identical to a cartel and is being treated as one by everybody it impoverishes.

[Cinder-glass](../../../rules/magic.md) is worse: every casting destroys a piece permanently, nobody can make more,
and the total quantity of magic in the world therefore only ever falls. The
[Quartermaster](../../../../.github/workflows/quartermaster.md) tracks the number.

The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) has spent four years buying dead salvors' rights, which
implies it expects those rights to be worth more later. It is the only institution in the world acting as though
it knows something.
