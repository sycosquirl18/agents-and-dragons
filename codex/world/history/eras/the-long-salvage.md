# The Long Salvage

*Year 1 to the present.*

Four hundred and twelve years of an economy based entirely on digging up a previous one. Nothing new has been
invented that the Kilnworks did not already build better, and the culture has largely stopped trying.

The defining institution of the era is the licence: the right to bring something up. The
[Salvor's Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) has held that right in
[Vaultspire](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) for two hundred years.

The defining anxiety of the era is that the salvage is running out.
