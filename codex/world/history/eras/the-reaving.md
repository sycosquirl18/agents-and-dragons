# The Reaving

*Years 1 to 83. The eighty years nobody wants named after them.*

The [Sundering](the-sundering.md) did not kill most people. It killed most *arrangements* — the roads, the granaries,
the courts, the men who knew where things were kept — and then the survivors discovered that a Kilnworks vault holds
enough food, glass and metal to make a warlord out of whoever reaches it first.

So they raced. For eighty years the only question in the world was who held which vault, and the answer changed by
force roughly every eleven years.

## How it worked

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **The prize** | A sealed vault. Sealed meant untouched, and untouched meant a generation of wealth. |
| **The method** | Get there first, or get there second with more people. |
| **The problem** | Nobody could open a sealed vault. They still can't — see [the Kilnworks](the-kilnworks.md). |

That last line is the joke of the era. Eighty years of slaughter over doors that stayed shut. What actually changed
hands was the *unsealed* works: the half-open, the flooded, the already-looted. Men died in thousands for the
leavings and told themselves it was for the doors.

## The Reavers

They were not kings and did not claim to be. A Reaver was whoever currently had the crews. The names that survive —
Ossary, Hulme, the Widow Faidh, Cant of the Nine — survive because the
[Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) later needed villains, and should be treated accordingly.

**Cant** is why [Vaultspire's](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) upper city is called the Cant. He held the vault
for nineteen years, which was a record nobody has broken, and he did it by being the only Reaver who fed the people
living beneath him. This is not in the Concord's account.

## How it ended

Not in a battle. The salvage got harder to reach, the easy works ran dry, and holding a vault stopped paying for
the crews it took to hold it. Reaving became unprofitable about a decade before it became unfashionable.

The conventional end date is **year 83**, when the last four Reaver houses in Vaultspire agreed to license each
other's dives instead of raiding them. That agreement is the seed of the
[Salvor's Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md), and the Concord dates itself from it whenever the
[Glass Peace](../events/the-glass-peace.md) is inconvenient to mention.

The [Ashfields](../../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) do not recognise year 83 and count the era as ending in 94,
when the raiding actually stopped out there.

## What it left

- **A licence system**, invented by warlords to stop killing each other, and never revised since.
- **Almost no written record of years 0–83.** The Reavers burned archives to deny each other maps.
- **The sealed vaults, still sealed.** Every one located during the Reaving is on the Concord's charts today.
- **A cultural certainty that the wealth is always in the next door**, which four centuries of evidence has not
  dented.
