# The Kilnworks

*Before year 0.*

An age that could work glass the way this one works iron, and built for it: vaults, kilns, and the roads between them,
all at a scale nobody since has attempted or needed. [Vaultspire](../../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) is squatting
inside one of their minor works.

No Kilnworks writing has ever been deciphered, though a great deal of it survives. It is not that the script is
unknown — it is that it appears to be a *notation*, and nobody can find what it is notating.

How long the era lasted is unknown. Estimates range from four centuries to forty.
