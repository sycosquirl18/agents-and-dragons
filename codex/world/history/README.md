# History

Time is counted from the Sundering. Everything before it is "the Kilnworks"; everything after is salvage.

| Era | Years | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Kilnworks](eras/the-kilnworks.md) | before 0 | The age that built the vaults and fired the glass, and then ended |
| [The Sundering](eras/the-sundering.md) | 0 | One day. The coast drowned, the Wound opened, the Kiln-lords went |
| [The Reaving](eras/the-reaving.md) | 1 – 83 | Eighty years of taking. The survivors ate the ruins and then each other |
| [The Long Salvage](eras/the-long-salvage.md) | 83 – present (412) | A licence, an economy, and four centuries of digging up a better age |

Specific moments live in [events/](events/README.md).

## Three warnings about all of this

1. **Almost nothing before year 100 was written by anyone who was there.** The Reaving burned more records than the
   Sundering did.
2. **Year counts are Concord counts.** [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) dates from the Sundering
   because the Concord finds that convenient. The [Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) date from the
   first harvest instead, are eleven years off, and will not be corrected.
3. **The history changed its mind in year 100.** See [The Hundred-Year Turn](events/the-hundred-year-turn.md).
   Every account written after it should be read as an argument.

Raw accounts of recent events are in the [Chronicle](../../chronicle/README.md); the
[Chronicler](../../../.github/workflows/chronicler.md) folds them up into these eras as they age.
