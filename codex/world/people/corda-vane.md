# Corda Vane

Grain-taster for the Long Room in [Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md) — the one who bites a handful from
every sack before it's weighed and says whether it'll keep. Forty years doing it; her thumb is worth more than
Cassardy's ledger.

**Wants:** someone to carry a warning she can't say out loud in the Long Room without naming a factor. She's found
shriveled grain — three years old, still in this season's sacks — in two of the granaries that stopped selling. Old
stock dressed as new. She'll say this much to anyone who buys her a meal and doesn't work for a grange.

**Knows:** which sacks are lying. Not why. She won't guess at the Covenant, the Concord, or the calendar — that's
[Enna Tallis](enna-tallis.md)'s argument, not hers — but she'll confirm the grain itself is old the moment someone
asks her to check, and she'll do it for free if it means the Long Room finally listens.

**Useful at the table:** a fast, cheap way to confirm the hoarding is real and has been going on longer than a
season, without spending a scene arguing with Cassardy. She doesn't know what the grain is being kept *for* — only
that it's being kept.
