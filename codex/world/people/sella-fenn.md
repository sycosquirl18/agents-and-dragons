# Sella Fenn

Net-mender and oddments trader in the lower market of [Catch](../geography/sites/catch.md), under the drying
racks near the ladder-foot. Twenty years of salvage lots have passed through her hands before anyone with standing
looked at them twice.

## What she has

A tin box of cinder-glass fragments too small or too cracked to sell, kept because each one is scratched with the
same recurring marks — the kind the [Kiln-lords' notation](../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md#the-notation-problem)
leaves. She has no idea what the marks mean. She knows they repeat, and that nobody at Vaultspire has ever wanted
to buy scrap that small before.

## What she wants

Money, mostly, and to stop being asked about the box. [Caude Renne](caude-renne.md) found out about it three
market days ago and has been raising his offer each time — well past what the glass is worth as scrap, not past
what a full set of matched notation would be worth to the [Ashen Covenant](../factions/ashen-covenant.md). She has
not said yes yet, but she has stopped saying no, and the next Turn is when he expects an answer.

## Useful at the table

- She sells straight salvage cheap and honestly — a way in for anyone new to Catch.
- She'll show the box to someone who asks the right way and doesn't threaten her; she has never shown it to anyone
  from Vaultspire before.
- If nobody outbids or talks her out of it, the box goes to Caude Renne at the next Turn — handing the Covenant a
  working sample of the notation before anyone else has one.
