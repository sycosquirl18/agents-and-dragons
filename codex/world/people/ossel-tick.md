# Ossel Tick

Former tide-caller in [Catch](../geography/sites/catch.md). Kept silent count of the four-minute tide drift
from year 409 until his death in summer 411, scratching each interval into
[The Tick-Weight](../items/the-tick-weight.md). Drowned in the lower Catch on a morning the Turn came early.
