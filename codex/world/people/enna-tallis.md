# Enna Tallis

Threshing's granary reckoner, in the room below the [Long Room](../geography/sites/threshing.md#the-long-room).
Forty years keeping the tally against a water-timepiece her grandmother built, marked in old notches nobody else
bothers reading anymore.

## What she wants

To be asked. She has counted the [eleven-year calendar discrepancy](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md#hooks)
against her timepiece for a decade and told two factors, who thanked her and did nothing. She will not volunteer it
a third time — she will answer, in full, if someone asks the right question.

## What she knows

Her timepiece runs against the moon-calendar Vaultspire and the Long Room both use for the season count. The drift
is small, deliberate-looking, and matches no natural cause she can name. She has never connected it to the
Saltmarch's [early tide](../geography/sites/catch.md) — nobody has told her that thread exists — but the numbers
would line up if she saw it.

She also keeps the real tallies for all eleven granaries, including the three now storing rather than selling. She
knows exactly how much grain is stacked and unsold, a figure the factors have not made public.

## At the table

- Costly to ask: getting her real tally numbers means admitting you don't trust Factor Cassardy's public count —
  word gets back to the Long Room.
- She will not say why the discrepancy matters, only that it does, and that nobody has ever brought her a second
  measurement to check it against.
