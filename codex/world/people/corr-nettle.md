# Corr Nettle

Ladder-warden at [Catch](../geography/sites/catch.md), answering to the Widow Sarn. Not named in her
household — hired help, workmanlike about it, no grudge and no mercy.

Corr stands at the single ladder wide enough for cargo and checks every load before it goes up: a marked chit from
the Widow, or it stays below at the Turn. No chit, no ride, no exceptions for a friendly face or a hard tide. He
funnels four hundred ladders' worth of traffic down to the one anyone official uses, and he has never once let it
through without the mark.

**Wants today:** his own chit back. He lent it to cover a debt weeks ago and the borrower has not returned it — Corr
cannot check anyone's marks against a mark he does not hold, and the Widow does not know it is missing yet.

**Obstacle:** anyone hauling cargo up from lower Catch without a Widow's chit has to get through Corr first, and he
does not bend for coin, threat, or a hero's reputation. Getting him to look away costs him something real — his
job, if the Widow finds out.

**Voice:** flat, procedural, repeats the rule rather than arguing it.
