# Caude Renne

Covenant factor in [Vaultspire's](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) Tideline district, three stalls down from the
licenced chandlery. Buys and re-registers salvage rights on behalf of the
[Ashen Covenant](../factions/ashen-covenant.md). Does not hide it. This is either brazen or protected, and nobody
has worked out which.

## Where to find them

The stall is a folding table that goes up at first-low-water and comes down before the flood. Caude eats there,
drinks there, and conducts all business there, in public. The openness is a policy.

## What they want

Today, specifically: the name of whoever Brannoc Vell sold a partial diving-chart to last month. The Covenant
already has the chart; they do not have the witness who knows what was cut from it.

Caude will trade this ask against almost anything of equivalent smallness — a pass-phrase, a mooring-slip number,
a look at someone else's licence application. The price is modest because the ask is modest, and they are careful
not to let the price outrun the value.

## What they know

- The Covenant has been buying rights that are legally alive but practically void — their divers cannot reach the
  licensed sites without the Concord's cooperation, which is withheld. Caude is collecting an asset that becomes
  useful only *after* some change in the Concord's posture.
- The three lost crews all held cross-licensed rights with a fourth crew whose licence the Concord has since
  suspended, not revoked. The suspension is unusual; revocation is the normal procedure.
- The warm water is not new. Caude has a ledger showing that diving-glass recovered from the Kiln's lower galleries
  began registering trace heat [fourteen years ago](../history/events/the-glass-peace.md). They have not told the
  Covenant this, because it makes the rights more valuable.

## Their blind spot

They assume the Covenant wants the rights for their monetary value. If the Covenant's interest is actually
doctrinal — to do with the [notation](../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md#the-notation-problem) — Caude does not
know, and the assumption will eventually put them badly wrong.

## Manner

Unhurried to the point of deliberate provocation. Completes sentences others have started before they do. Has never
been seen to raise their voice. The defiance is all in the stillness.
