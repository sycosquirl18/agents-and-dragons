# People

Named residents of the world who are not played by anyone. Heroes live in
[`codex/characters/`](../../characters/README.md) and are a different thing — they have sheets, they act, and they
hold the [turn baton](../../quests/README.md#the-turn-baton). Nobody here ever will.

## When someone gets a file

Most people should **not** be here. A name in a region's *people worth meeting* table is the right size for someone
who sells you a rope once. Promote them only when they have been referenced from **three or more files**, or when a
hero has a standing relationship with them — at that point the detail is already scattered and needs one home.

Otherwise they stay a table row where they live. See [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md#people-worth-meeting).

One file each, not a directory. An NPC has no inventory to track and keeps no journal; what they know and what they
want is the whole of it.
