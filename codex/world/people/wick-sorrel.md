# Wick Sorrel

Wool-and-tallow broker at the wagon yard in [Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md), the Ashfields. Sells
greased wool cording, tallow candles, and waxed rope to anyone passing the [Kiln Road](../geography/sites/the-kiln-road.md)
east or west — divers included, because greased wool holds a knot underwater longer than hemp does.

## What she wants

Her wagon back, or its worth in coin. [Gault Marrow](../../characters/gault-marrow/sheet.md) won it off her at
dice two seasons ago and has been circling the Ashfields with it ever since instead of settling — she has heard
he is now unwelcome at three granges, which she takes as proof he will be back through Threshing looking for a
fourth, and she means to be standing at the yard gate when he is.

## What she knows

The granges are not only storing grain this season — two of the three that stopped selling have also stopped
selling wool, and she buys wool. Nobody outside the Long Room has connected the two yet. She has said nothing to
[Factor Elm Cassardy](../geography/sites/threshing.md#people), because a broker who talks herself out of a supply
line talks herself out of business.

## At the table

Useful to anyone passing through Threshing who needs rope, wax, or a candle that won't gutter in a flooded gallery
— she is not particular about a licence. Also useful to anyone chasing Gault Marrow: she will trade what she knows
about the granges' wool-hoarding for help collecting her debt, and she has heard exactly where his wagon has been
seen. She will not wait patiently; if nobody helps her before he next passes through, she means to try collecting
it herself, and she is not a fighter.
