# Josk Hallin

Ladder-boss of [Catch](../geography/sites/catch.md), answering to the Widow Sarn. He decides which of the four
hundred rope ladders go up first at the Turn, and in what order — which means he decides who reaches the upper
town in time and who does not.

Josk has counted the same four minutes Tallow Ashe and Marra Whit have, and he trusts neither of their reasons for
it. His answer is not to ask why the water is early. His answer is to start hauling the ladders four minutes
before Tallow's bell, on his own reckoning, so that nobody is caught below on a rumour he cannot verify. He has
not told the Widow. He has not told Tallow. He means to do it at the next Turn regardless.

This will maroon whoever is still on the lower lanes when he starts early — traders packing up, latecomers off
the [Kiln Road](../geography/sites/the-kiln-road.md), anyone who trusted the bell over the ladder-boss. It will
also end Catch's four-minutes-early secret the hard way, in public, at the worst possible moment for
[Marra Whit](marra-whit.md), who means to ring the old warning bell herself on her own schedule.

**Obstacle:** getting up the ladders in time — to Josk, on his clock, not the bell's.
**What he wants:** to be blamed for nothing. He would rather strand fifty people than be the ladder-boss who let
one drown on a schedule he had stopped believing.
**What stops him:** the Widow Sarn owns the ladders. He does not. If she orders him to wait for the bell, he will
— but she has not been asked, and nobody currently knows to ask her.
