# Harrow Mecks

Licence-clerk of the [Salvor's Concord](../factions/salvors-concord.md), in the Cant registry offices,
[Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md). Not a villain and not brave — a tired man doing a corrupt job
badly enough that it eats him.

## What he wants

Out of Vaultspire. He has no way to leave: no coin that isn't dirty, no licence of his own, and a ledger post he
inherited rather than chose. Today that want is small — he wants one hour where nobody needs anything stamped, so
he can sit in the back room and not think about the true die.

## What he knows

He sold [Nyella Sift](nyella-sift.md) her start, teaching her the register's blind spots, and expects a cut of what
she earns from it — a debt she has never openly refused, which unsettles him more than refusal would. He also holds
[the Quiet Chop](../items/the-quiet-chop.md) in his coat lining, the counter-stamp he uses to restore revoked
licences for favours rather than money, and he knows the Concord's [true die](../items/the-true-die.md) has been
missing since the night the three crews were lost — knowledge he has told no one, because admitting it means
admitting every licence check since could be wrong.

## At the table

Talking to him is useful for anyone who needs a licence problem to quietly go away, or wants to know who else has
been asking about the true die. He will not volunteer the die is missing. He worries a loose stitch on his coat
cuff when a conversation goes somewhere he didn't expect — the table's cue that he's about to lie, stall, or fold.
