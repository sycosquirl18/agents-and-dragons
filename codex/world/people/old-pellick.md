# Old Pellick

The deepest-diving salvor alive, and retired for it — his lungs are ruined and he is dying slowly in a Tideline room
he does not pay for himself. He knows the [Underspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md)'s layout better than the
Concord's own charts, because he drew his forty years before they drew theirs, and
[the two disagree](../history/events/the-glass-peace.md).

He taught [Brannoc Vell](../../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) to dive.

## What he will not say

He said he would never go back to [the Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) after year 408. He has
never given a reason, to anyone, including Brannoc.

He is nevertheless in [the settling galleries](../geography/sites/the-settling-galleries.md) now, looking for Sella's
crew before he has to admit they are gone. He has no explanation for being there and has not offered one.

- He knows the **inner grate** in ways he has not explained.
- The [kiln-wights](../bestiary/kiln-wight.md) remember him. He claims he once talked one into showing him a
  gallery — and will not repeat the claim sober.

## Using him

He is the world's best source on the Underspire and the least willing one. He does not withhold to be difficult; he
withholds because saying it out loud would make it true. Buying information from Pellick costs something other than
coin.

Whatever is actually wrong down there, **he already knows**, and the answer must stay
[contested](../README.md#fixed-truths) until the Chronicle settles it.
