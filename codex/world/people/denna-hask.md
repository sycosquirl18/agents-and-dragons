# Denna Hask

Tackle-monger in [Tideline](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md#districts), Vaultspire. Sells the rope, weighted
belts, and breathing reeds a diver needs to go under the Underspire without a Concord licence — none of it
illegal to own, only to use for what most of her customers use it for.

Her stall is built from the timbers of three wrecks that came apart on the Saltmarch bar, which she will tell you
about at length if you let her, and which is why half her stock looks older than it is.

## What she wants

To stop being owed. [Old Pellick](old-pellick.md) taught her father the deep dives before his lungs went, and when
her father died under the waterline it was Pellick's rope that came back up empty. He has outfitted her stall for
free ever since without being asked and without saying why, and she has stopped being able to tell if it is
kindness or penance. She wants him to say which, once, and has never asked.

## What she knows

- Which divers are buying gear for the Underspire this week, and roughly how deep they mean to go, because the
  tackle tells her — long lines and reed bundles mean galleries, not the shallows.
- Pellick's old charts by feel; she has re-coiled and re-oiled the ropes on them for years without reading them.

## Useful at the table

- Sells diving gear on credit to anyone Pellick has quietly vouched for, no questions, no licence checked.
- Will not say a word against Pellick, and changes the subject hard if pressed on why he supplies her for free.
- Knows who is diving the Kiln's galleries this tide by what they bought from her — a fast way to learn who's down
  there without asking the Concord.
