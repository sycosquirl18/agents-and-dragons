# Dovie Wrack

Catch's death-teller, in the lower town under the drying racks. When the tide gives back a body — or a diver
comes up wrong — Dovie is who listens to the chest before anyone asks questions. She learned it from nobody;
she learned it from thirty years of bodies.

She'll hear anyone's breathing for a coin, divers included, and tell them plainly whether the next dive kills
them. She does not soften it. [Old Pellick](old-pellick.md) never let her listen to his — the one patient she
never got to finish.

She works under the drying racks because the mud there never fully dries — the lower town's muck holds the
smell of a body longer than clean stone would, and Dovie has learned to read that too: a corpse fresh off the
tide smells of brine and rot, like any drowning should. Every one she's checked this year, living or dead,
carries a second smell underneath it — scorched lime — the same the roof pocket in
[the gallery below the passage](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md#the-gallery-below-the-passage) breathes
out. She has never been to the Kiln. She has no way to know that, and has said nothing about it because she
doesn't have the other half of the pattern to say it *to*.

## What she knows

Every drowned or ruined-lunged body she's checked this past year has the same wrongness: congestion like a
kiln-worker's, not a diver's — a pulmonary crackle low in the chest, worse on the side that was down longer.
Cold-water drowning scars the chest one way; this is another — the kind she'd expect from breathing something
hot and thick, not swallowing brine. She has said nothing, because saying it means naming what's warming the
water, and that is a Concord question, not a death-teller's.

## What is hidden

She keeps a jar of dried rack-muck by her stool, packed with the lime-smelling silt she scrapes from every
body before washing it — thirty years of habit, never asked why. Wits DC 14 to notice the jar is full of the
same substance on every corpse regardless of where in the Saltmarch they drowned, which means whatever leaves
that residue is not local to any one drowning site. `stub`

## Useful at the table

- Will examine a diver's chest and give an honest verdict on whether they can dive again — Grace or Wits check
  to convince her otherwise if the answer is no.
- Holds the pattern above as a costly truth: she'll trade it for something she wants more than her quiet, or
  give it up under real pressure, but not for coin.
- Ties into [The Kiln Still Burns](../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md) without settling what fires the kilns —
  she has the symptom, not the cause.

## Hooks

- The scorched-lime smell under every body she's checked matches [the Unspooled Line](../items/the-unspooled-line.md)
  and the Kiln's roof pocket exactly — a thread nobody at Catch has any reason to connect to the Kiln yet. `stub`
- Her jar of scraped residue is physical evidence of what's heating the water, sitting unexamined under the
  drying racks while the Concord and the Covenant both look elsewhere. `stub`
