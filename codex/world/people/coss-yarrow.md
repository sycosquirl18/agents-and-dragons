# Coss Yarrow

Ashfield Grange enforcer who has set himself up four days east of
[Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md), on the [Kiln Road](../geography/sites/the-kiln-road.md), exactly
where wagon traffic thins out and stops. He calls it a toll. The [granges](../factions/ashfield-granges.md) call
it nothing at all, because nobody sent him.

## What he wants

To be the reason nothing moves east or west past day four without his say-so. Two granges have stopped selling
grain and wool both, and Coss has appointed himself the man who counts what still tries to cross — wagons, wool,
strayed stock, anyone's business but his own. He takes a cut in kind, not coin, and writes nothing down.

## What he knows

Which wagons have passed east this season and what they carried, because he has stopped every one. He knows
[Gault Marrow](../../characters/gault-marrow/sheet.md) came through with a won wagon and turned back rather than
pay the toll — Coss let him go rather than fight for it, and has been telling people since that the wagon owes
him twice now. He does not know why two granges are hoarding wool as well as grain; he only knows they stopped
paying him to look away from their wagons around the same time, which he takes personally rather than as a clue.

## At the table

An obstacle, not a villain: he will let a party pass for a toll paid in kind — rope, tools, information, a name —
and turns genuinely dangerous only if refused outright or if someone tries to walk past him. No grange sent him
and no grange will back him if it costs them standing with [Factor Elm Cassardy](../geography/sites/threshing.md#the-long-room);
anyone who checks that in Threshing can have him withdrawn, but has to be the one to do the checking.
