# Dorcas Weel

Rota-warden at [the Weeping Bore](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md), and the
[Salvor's Concord](../factions/salvors-concord.md)'s only permanent presence there. She logs which crew cuts which
face, at which level, and countersigns every licence before a lamp goes down the shaft.

She keeps the level-5 rota board and the level-6 rota board both, though only one of them has crews written on it.
Level 6's peg has sat uncrossed since 408 and she re-chalks its line every tenday out of habit, not instruction —
nobody has told her to stop, and nobody above her seems to remember it is still issued.

## Wants

Today, something small: a witnessed signature on a level-5 rota swap two crews have already agreed between
themselves. Concord process requires her countersign in person, and she has been waiting since dawn for either
crew's second to show up and make it official. She will talk to anyone willing to fetch one.

## Knows

- Exactly who was on rota, and where, the day the three crews were lost at [the Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) — it isn't her jurisdiction, but she keeps the habit of checking every board she's ever been handed.
- The level-6 licence was never formally revoked, only left off every crew assignment since 408. She has asked twice, in writing, whether to stop chalking it. No answer either time.
- Which two crews at level 5 have started trading shifts quietly rather than filing through her — technically against Concord rule, and she hasn't reported it yet.

## Voice

Precise, unhurried, faintly disappointed in everyone's paperwork. Answers questions with the rota board's actual language before her own opinion.
