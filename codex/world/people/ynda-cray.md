# Ynda Cray

Keeps the debt-book at [Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md)'s wagon yard, unofficially, because Hesk's own
records are a tangle of crossed-out names and she is the only one who can still read them back ten years. Wagoners,
factors, and grange seconds all come to her before they come to Hesk, because Hesk will quote you the ledger and
Ynda will tell you what it actually means.

**Wants today:** her stylus nib has snapped and Threshing's one tinker left for the Kiln Road last season. A
traveller with a decent blade and a steady hand could cut her a replacement in an afternoon — she pays in
information, not coin.

**What she knows:** she has alphabetized every account that has passed through the yard since before the Long Room
started storing rather than selling. She noticed the pattern before [Wick Sorrel](wick-sorrel.md) did — two
granges have been settling their wagon-yard debts early and in full, all season, which is not something a grange
does unless it expects not to need credit again soon.

She will not name the granges to a stranger. She will confirm or deny a name someone else offers her.
