# The Salvor's Concord

The guild that issues diving licences in [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md), and through them controls
every legal route from the seabed to the market. Two hundred years old, formed originally as a burial society for
drowned salvors, which is still technically its charter.

## Wants

To keep the licence system, because the licence system is the Concord. Its deeper motive is scarcity management: the
Concord has known for at least a generation that the accessible salvage is running out, and its strategy is to slow
extraction so the decline arrives after everyone currently in charge is dead.

## Wrong about

That this is a secret. Every experienced salvor in Tideline has worked it out. The Concord's authority is already
running on fumes and its leadership cannot see it.

## Currently

Closed the [Drowned Kiln](../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md) to diving after three crews were lost, citing tide
risk. The tide risk that month was ordinary.

## Levers

- **Harrow Mecks**, licence-clerk, sells the register to anyone who asks nicely and in coin.
- A revoked licence can be restored — the price is a favour, never money.
- The Concord cannot police the Underspire at neap tide and knows it.
