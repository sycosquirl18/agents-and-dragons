# Factions

| Faction | Seat | Wants |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Salvor's Concord](salvors-concord.md) | [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) | To control who may bring salvage up, forever |
| [The Ashen Covenant](ashen-covenant.md) | Chapterhouses everywhere | To be there when the Kiln-lords come back |
| [The Ashfield Granges](ashfield-granges.md) | [The Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) | A fair price for grain, by force if necessary |

Every faction needs a stated **want** that can put it in a hero's way, and something it is **wrong about**. A faction
that is simply correct and competent is scenery, not a faction.
