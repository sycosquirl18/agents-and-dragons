# The Ashen Covenant

A faith built on the fact that the Kiln-lords left no bodies. If they did not die, they went somewhere; if they went
somewhere, they can return. The Covenant exists to be ready, useful, and — crucially — *owed* when they do.

Chapterhouses in most towns of any size, a large one in the Cant in
[Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md), and almoners everywhere there are poor people. Funded by tithes,
by legacies, and by a great deal of quiet property dealing.

## Wants

Everything the Kilnworks made or wrote **before year 100**. Objects, glass, and above all documents — see
[the Hundred-Year Turn](../history/events/the-hundred-year-turn.md) for why that year is the line.

It is not collecting relics. It is assembling a *case*: proof of what the Kiln-lords were owed, by whom, and for
what, ready to be presented to them. The Covenant is, in its own understanding, a creditor waiting on a debtor who
is merely late.

## Wrong about

**Its own age.** The Covenant believes it preserves the original reading of the Sundering. Its founding documents
postdate the Turn. Everything it calls original is the revision — it was founded *on* the new account and has never
seriously entertained that the account was new.

Which makes the document hunt dangerous to the hunter. Four years and a fortune have gone into finding pre-Turn
writing, and nobody senior has asked out loud what the Covenant does with one that contradicts it.

Underneath that, a second error nobody raises: the whole faith assumes that whatever comes back would recognise a
debt, a chapterhouse, or a person. The [wights](../bestiary/kiln-wight.md) are the closest thing to a Kilnworks
survivor anyone has met, and they recognise nothing.

## Currently

- **Buying dead salvors' inherited rights** around Vaultspire for four years, at prices that make no commercial
  sense — including around the [Drowned Kiln](../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md). Publicly: caring for widows.
- **Bought [the Last Lens](../history/events/the-last-lens.md) in 408**, the same year the rights-buying began,
  for a sum the [Concord](salvors-concord.md) has never been able to source. It has not been spent.
- **Asked [Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md) for the six pre-year-100 documents four times.** Refused
  three times. The fourth was not phrased as a request and has not yet been answered.
- **Walks the rim of [the Glass Wound](../geography/regions/the-glass-wound.md)** and does not cut. Nobody outside
  the order knows what the walkers are counting.

## Levers

- **Sister Aude**, almoner in Tideline. Feeds the poor honestly and counts them exactly. Both are real.
- **Brother Idren** at Threshing — popular, patient, and the one who keeps asking.
- The chapterhouse library holds **at least two pre-Turn documents** and is closed to outsiders. This is the single
  most valuable unread thing in the known world.
- The Covenant pays in **cleared debts, food, and favours**, rarely in coin. A hero who takes its help has taken a
  position, and it will say so eventually.
- It has never once refused a beggar. This is not a tactic, which is why it works.
