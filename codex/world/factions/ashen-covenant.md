# The Ashen Covenant

A faith built on the fact that the Kiln-lords left no bodies. If they did not die, they went somewhere; if they went
somewhere, they can return. The Covenant exists to be ready, useful, and — crucially — *owed* when they do.

Funded by tithes and by a great deal of quiet property dealing. It has been buying dead salvors' inherited rights
around [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) for four years at prices that make no commercial sense.

Popular with the poor, who it feeds honestly, and distrusted by the [Concord](salvors-concord.md), which cannot work
out what it is accumulating.
