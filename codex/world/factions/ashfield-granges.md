# The Ashfield Granges

The grain cartel of [the Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md). Not a government — an agreement between
about forty families that has held for a century, mostly.

They have noticed that [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) cannot eat without them and that they can eat
without Vaultspire.
