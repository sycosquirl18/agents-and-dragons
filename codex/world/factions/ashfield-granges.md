# The Ashfield Granges

The grain cartel of [the Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md). Not a government — an agreement between
about forty families that has held for a century, mostly. Eleven of them send factors to the Long Room at
[Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md), where the price is set for the season and everyone else takes it.

They learned the technique from the [Glass Peace](../history/events/the-glass-peace.md) and will tell you so. They
regard this as evidence of good sense rather than of anything else.

## Wants

Never again to be at the mercy of a city. The granges were walled during
[the Reaving](../history/eras/the-reaving.md) and have never been unwalled, which is the whole faction in one fact.
A fair price is the stated want; the real one is the ability to *refuse* — and they have noticed that
[Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) cannot eat without them and that they can eat without Vaultspire.

## Wrong about

**That withholding is safe.** They have modelled what a hungry Vaultspire pays. They have not modelled what a
starving one does, and it has an armed [Concord](salvors-concord.md), two hundred thousand people, and nowhere else
on the map to go.

**And about the documents.** The granges hold six pieces of writing from before year 100 and treat them as proof
that the [orthodox history](../history/events/the-hundred-year-turn.md) is a Vaultspire invention. They may well be
right. But the last full reading was three generations ago, and what everyone out here quotes is that reader's
notes, not the documents. The strongbox is opened for inventory, not for study.

## Currently

- **Three granges have stopped selling entirely.** Factor Elm Cassardy will not say why and is not bluffing.
- Storing rather than selling for two seasons — see [economy](../../rules/economy.md) for what that does to bread.
- Sitting on a fourth [Covenant](ashen-covenant.md) approach for the documents that was not phrased as a request.

## Levers

- **Factor Elm Cassardy** speaks for the Long Room. Courteous, immovable, and privately worried.
- Grange law is whatever the grange says, and there is no other court out there. This cuts both ways for a hero
  who is useful.
- They trade in grain, wagons, and standing — not coin, which they have and do not respect.
- Anyone arriving from Vaultspire is a Concord buyer until proven otherwise. Proving otherwise is a way in.
