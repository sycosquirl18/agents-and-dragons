# The Drawn Snuffer

**What it is:** a candle-snuffer the length of a hand, its bell drawn out thin and gilded at the rim, the handle
stamped with the Ashen Covenant's sunburst. It is not for a candle in a room — it is for the taper a Covenant factor
lights over a dead salvor's rights-of-claim before signing them away, snuffed the instant the ink is dry so the
smoke, not the flame, is what the widow keeps.

**Bearing:** None — it is ceremony, not equipment.

**Where it is:** [Caude Renne's](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) stall in Tideline, kept in a felt-lined box
under the counter. He has drawn it out and snuffed a taper over eleven contracts in four years, buying dead
salvors' inherited rights around Vaultspire — including, this season, rights near the
[Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md). He offers the same small ceremony to everyone he buys from,
which is either kindness or a very good habit for a man whose employer wants the paper more than the coin.

**Worth:** Not for sale — Renne would not part with it, and no smith would take the commission twice; the sunburst
stamp is the Covenant's own and marks anything cut with it as chapterhouse property.

Cut in year 408, the same year the [Covenant bought the Last Lens](../history/events/the-last-lens.md) and began
buying rights around Vaultspire at prices the [Concord](../factions/salvors-concord.md) has never been able to
source. Nobody has asked Renne, out loud, whether the ceremony is meant to comfort the widow or to make certain
she signs before she thinks better of it.
