# The Quiet Chop

**What it is:** a brass counter-stamp the size of a thumb, cut with the Salvor's Concord's licence-seal in reverse —
but engraved half a hair narrower than the register die, so a renewal stamped with it never quite matches the
master book. Nobody has ever checked closely enough to notice.

**Bearing:** None — it is not a weapon or a tool, it is a forgery instrument. If used, treat any licence it marks as
genuine to every eye but a direct comparison against the Concord's register in Vaultspire.

**Where it is:** In [Harrow Mecks'](../factions/salvors-concord.md#levers) coat lining. It is how he restores revoked
licences for a favour instead of coin — he was never selling the register itself, he was selling five minutes alone
with this.

**Worth:** Not for sale. A Concord clerk caught holding it would hang for it; a rival clerk would kill to own it
outright and cut Mecks out of his own trade.

A coppersmith cut it in year 398 for a clerk who has since died — not Mecks, his predecessor, who wanted a way to
waive the fee for salvors he owed. Mecks inherited the stamp along with the ledger post and has been more careful
with it than his teacher ever was, using it perhaps once a season, always for a favour that outweighs the risk of
someone laying a genuine renewal next to a Chopped one and noticing the die doesn't sit flush.
