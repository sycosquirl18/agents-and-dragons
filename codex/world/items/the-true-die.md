# The True Die

**What it is:** A block of dull, light-swallowing iron the size of a thumb, cast two centuries ago for the
[Salvor's Concord's](../factions/salvors-concord.md) founding vault-warden. It is the master counter-stamp every
licence in [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) is checked against — not a licence itself, but the thing
that makes a licence real. [The Quiet Chop](the-quiet-chop.md), [Harrow Mecks'](../people/harrow-mecks.md) forged counter-stamp, is a hair off
this die because whoever cut it never held the true one to copy.

**Bearing:** Advantage on any check to forge or pass off Concord paperwork as genuine — a licence, a revocation
notice, a manifest — while the die is in hand. Its flaw: every strike leaves a faint double edge, a smith's flaw
two hundred years uncorrected, and any Concord clerk who has stamped a thousand licences will notice it on the
second look, not the first.

**Where it is:** Missing from the Concord's Cant vault since the night three crews were lost in
[the Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) — taken in the confusion of an unscheduled cargo
unloading at the Tideline docks, the same week licences started getting revoked without hearings. Nobody has
reported it gone; admitting the true die is missing would mean admitting every licence check since could be
wrong. [Nyella Sift](../../characters/nyella-sift/sheet.md) has it now, which is why her forged paperwork has
started passing where it shouldn't.

**Worth:** Not for sale. Holding it unlicensed is a hanging offence in the Cant; the Concord has never had to write
the law because no one but a vault-warden was ever meant to touch it.
