# The Tick-Weight

**What it is:** A brass tide-counting weight the size of a closed fist, flat on two faces and hexagonal on the
rest, used for calibrating the water-clocks that govern the Turn in [Catch](../geography/sites/catch.md). It is
heavier than it looks and smells faintly of brine even after drying. Someone has scratched forty-one hash-marks
into its lower edge in sets of four minutes — and then stopped mid-set, the last mark unfinished.

**Bearing:** None mechanical. But it is the only record anyone has made of the drift — forty-one counts over
three years, always the same: four minutes early. Whoever holds it holds proof the tide is not where the charts
say it is.

**Worth:** Nothing at market. A great deal to [Tallow Ashe](../geography/sites/catch.md#people), who keeps it
out of sight. More still to whoever wants to understand the
[four-minute anomaly](../geography/sites/catch.md#dangers) without asking Tallow directly.

**Flaw:** Every mariner who worked under [Ossel Tick](../people/ossel-tick.md) would recognise the weight on
sight — it hung from the clock-post at the top of the tide-stairs for eleven years. Carrying it out of Catch
asks questions. Selling it is worse.

**Where it is:** On a shelf in Tallow Ashe's croft, upper Catch, behind a coil of line and a broken compass
rose. It is not hidden. Tallow believes it looks unremarkable to everyone but himself.

---

Ossel Tick was the tide-caller before Tallow. He started noting the drift in year 409, said nothing because he
thought his clock was faulty, replaced the clock, and kept counting. He died in the summer of 411, chest-deep
in the lower Catch on a morning the Turn was early and the ladders were up. His tools went to Tallow, who
understood the hash-marks on the third day.

Tallow has told no one because he does not know whether the problem is the moon, the Kiln, or something he
will be blamed for. He rings the Turn four minutes early because he trusts the weight more than the charts.
He has not shown the weight to anyone because showing it means explaining Ossel, and explaining Ossel means
explaining why he waited a year to speak.
