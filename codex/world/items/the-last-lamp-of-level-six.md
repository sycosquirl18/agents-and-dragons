# The Last Lamp of Level Six

**What it is:** A cutter's hand-lamp, [Weeping Bore](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md) pattern — a fist-sized
iron cage around a wick that should have burned out years ago and has not. The glass is scorched black on one side,
as if someone shielded it from view rather than let it go dark. It is warm to hold, always, even wrapped and cold-stored.

**Bearing:** Lit, it counts as one of the [three lamps](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md#the-rule-about-the-dark)
the Bore's dark-rule requires — but it cannot be unlit. Wick, oil, and flame are the same object; smothering, dousing,
or starving it of air does nothing. Whoever carries it lit is granting the safety of three lamps to a crew of one,
which is generous, and also means it never stops burning where someone can see it.

**Where it is:** The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) chapterhouse in the Cant, catalogued and
unseen since 408 — the year [Level 6](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md) was sunk and, the same season, the
[Last Lens](../history/events/the-last-lens.md) was bought at auction. The Covenant will not confirm it holds a
Bore-crew lamp at all, which is itself the tell.

**Worth:** Not for sale. The Covenant paid to make Level 6 idle and paid again to keep this quiet; a hero seen with
it is a hero the Covenant will move on directly, not through an intermediary.

## Provenance

Struck for the last crew to work Level 6 before the shaft went idle in 408 "by whose order" the Bore's own rota
still won't say. The foreman of that crew, Dessa Whit, is not on any manifest of the missing — the Concord's Level 6
records simply stop the week the lamp does. Nobody has asked the Covenant why an almoner's ledger from that year
lists a payment for "assurance, one shaft, ongoing," and the Covenant has not volunteered that it does.

The lamp reads as ordinary Bore-issue iron and glass, not cinder-glass — nothing about it is Kilnworks-made. What it
burns on, four years unfed, is the same open question as the warm water at [the Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md)
and the weeping at the Bore itself: nobody has found the source, and it does its thing anyway.
