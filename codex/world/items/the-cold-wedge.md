# The Cold Wedge

**What it is:** A hand-tool the length of a forearm, single-edged, cast from a grey metal that takes no rust and
holds no warmth even this close to the firing floor — nothing like the bronze and iron the [Kilnworks](../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md)
otherwise favoured. The grip end is worn to a mirror by hands that are not there to explain it. It was made for
grading, not fighting: a [Kiln-lord](../README.md) tool for splitting raw ore before it went to the sorting
channels below.

**Bearing:** Advantage on one Might check per scene to force, pry, or shear a metal or stone fixture — a locked
shackle, a rusted hasp, a jammed grate. It goes through calcified lock-wire the way a hot knife goes through
tallow.

**Flaw:** The grip was shaped for a Kiln-lord's hand, not a human one, and four centuries of use have polished it
past any purchase. Wet, cold, or rushed, it wants to twist free mid-swing — treat any fumble while using it as
the Wedge slipping, not the target holding. Grace DC 11 to keep hold of it on a bad roll.

**Where it is:** Wedged into the passage wall at the chest-width squeeze before [the settling galleries'](../geography/sites/the-settling-galleries.md)
final approach to the inner grate — left standing upright in fresh stone, exactly where someone used it to cut
the new handholds noted there. Whoever left it has not come back for it.

**Worth:** Not for sale — no smith in [Vaultspire](../geography/regions/vaultspire.md) can identify the metal,
and a Kilnworks tool with no maker's mark draws Concord attention before it draws coin.

---

Nothing living has cast metal like this since year 0, which means either the Kiln-lords left more behind than
anyone has found, or something has been making tools in their manner more recently than anyone would like to
believe. The hand-cut new holds in the passage wall were not there last season. Someone came through with this
Wedge, cut their way to the inner grate, and left the tool behind mid-job — the kind of thing a person does when
they mean to come straight back, or when they no longer can.
