# The Left Tally

**What it is:** A lead tag the size of a thumbnail, threaded on waxed cord and worn under a diver's collar, never
in the open. Salvor superstition holds that before a dive you scratch one name into it — whoever you'd want told
first if you didn't come up — and you leave the tag on the body of the one who does the telling. This one is
scratched with a single word, *Pellick*, in a hand that pressed too hard on the last letter.

**Bearing:** None — it is a promise, not a tool.

**Where it is:** Still on its cord around the neck of the diver Brannoc Vell found wedged past the inner grate of
[the Drowned Kiln](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md) — see
[412-214, A Body at the Grate](../../chronicle/412-214-a-body-at-the-grate.md). Nobody has cut it free. The body
has no wound, no held tool, and no explanation, but the tally was clearly meant to be delivered, not lost.

**Worth:** Not for sale. A tally is worthless to anyone but the name scratched on it, and worth everything to that
one person.

---

Salvors don't talk about the custom to outsiders, and licensed crews don't log it — the [Concord](../factions/salvors-concord.md)
considers it morbid and bad for recruitment. [Old Pellick](../people/old-pellick.md) taught it to Brannoc along
with everything else he taught him, and has never once said whose tag he's carried, or whether he still does.
Whoever this diver was, they meant Pellick to be told before anyone else — and Pellick is currently in
[the settling galleries](../geography/sites/the-settling-galleries.md), looking for this crew, still not knowing
one of them is already found.
