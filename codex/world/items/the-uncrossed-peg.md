# The Uncrossed Peg

**What it is:** A brass tally-peg the length of a finger, stamped `VI` and threaded on a loop of tarred cord. It
hangs on [the Weeping Bore's](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md) rota board in the Concord's shed at the
shaft-head, in the row for Level 6, where every other peg has long since been crossed off and pocketed. This one
has hung invariable since 408 — four years of salt fog and nobody has taken it down.

**Bearing:** None. It is a job someone is owed and nobody will claim: whoever pulls the Uncrossed Peg is, by the
Bore's unwritten custom, declaring themselves the crew of record for Level 6 — first claim on a licence nobody has
cancelled, and first blame if anyone asks who authorised the shaft being idle this long.

**Where it is:** Still on the rota board, [the Weeping Bore](../geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md), shaft-head
shed. The clerk who should have crossed it off in 408 is gone from the roster; nobody currently posted knows his
name.

**Worth:** Not for sale. Its only value is the claim it carries, and claiming it costs a favour or a fight with
whoever else has been eyeing that same silence.

---

Rota clerks cross a peg the day a level is stood down, in front of a Concord witness, and the crew's names go into
the ledger as released. Level 6 was never stood down — it was simply not worked, the morning after
[the Last Lens](../history/events/the-last-lens.md), and the peg for it was never touched because nobody could say
who had the authority to touch it. Four years of shaft-head fog have pitted the brass but not moved it an inch.

Pulling it does not open the level. It only makes one thing true that was not true a moment before: somebody is
now responsible for finding out why it was left shut.
