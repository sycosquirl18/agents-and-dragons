# The Waiting Stone

**What it is:** A ballast stone the size of two fists, cut cleaner and paler than every other stone in
[the drowned gallery row](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md#the-gallery-below-the-passage) — no barnacle
growth, no silt stain, edges still sharp from the chisel. Scratched into its face is the same tally notation as
the rest of the row's stones, one count higher than any body yet weighted there.

**Bearing:** None — it is evidence, not a tool. Anyone who lays it beside the row and counts sees the tally is
short by exactly one, matching [the gallery's hidden ledger](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md#what-is-hidden).

**Where it is:** At the foot of the row in the gallery below the passage slot, cut and set aside before
[Ress Adair](../geography/sites/the-drowned-kiln.md#the-gallery-below-the-passage) was placed there, still unused
— she has not yet been weighted down.

**Worth:** Not for sale. It only means something in the room it was cut for.

It was not cut in a hurry. Wherever the kiln-wights keep their tally stones waiting, this one sat finished before
Ress Adair went missing from Sella's crew — carved for a count that had not happened yet, by whatever keeps the
row's inventory. Taking it out of the gallery proves the row was expecting her before anyone else knew she was
gone; leaving it in place means the wights finish their knot on schedule.

## Hooks

- Whoever carves these stones knows who is coming before Vaultspire's registries do. `stub`
- If the stone is removed before the tally is checked against it, the wights may notice the count is missing a
  *stone*, not just a body. `stub`
