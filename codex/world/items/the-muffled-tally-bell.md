# The Muffled Tally-Bell

**What it is:** A grange watch-bell, the kind that hangs over a granary door so a
[tally-daw](../bestiary/tally-daw.md) can knock it with its beak when a stranger comes near. This one has been
wrapped, clapper and rim both, in wax-stiffened cloth — carefully, by a hand that knew exactly how much sound it
needed to kill.

**Bearing:** Useful. Carried and struck deliberately, it grants advantage on one check to move unheard past a
sleeping household or watch at night — the ring that should carry for a quarter mile instead reaches nobody. But
the wax is a single use: strike it twice in one night and the cloth splits, the bell rings true and loud on the
second blow, and everyone within earshot knows exactly where to look.

**Where it is:** In Hesk's wagon yard at [Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md), hung on a nail behind the
tack he does not sell, where he can reach it without being seen reaching.

**Worth:** Not for sale. Hesk would deny owning it before he'd name a price.

Three granges stopped selling to Vaultspire and started storing — see
[the Ashfields](../geography/regions/the-ashfields.md) and [Threshing](../geography/sites/threshing.md#hooks). Grain
does not move itself into a granary at night without a bell that stays quiet, and Hesk sells the wheels. He wrapped
this one himself, the first night the wagons ran dark, and has not said so to Factor Cassardy or to anyone else. He
keeps it in case the wagons need to run again before the Long Room decides it's ready to be seen.
