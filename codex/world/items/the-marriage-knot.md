# The Marriage Knot

**What it is:** A yard of waxed cord tied with eleven measuring-knots, the kind a tailor keeps instead of a
tape because a knot does not stretch with the weather. Every knot is spaced for a different garment: cuff,
collar, waist. The eleventh knot is tied wrong on purpose — too close to the tenth to mean any measurement at all.

**Bearing:** None — it is just old and it was somebody's.

**Where it is:** In [Mabb Orrin's](../geography/sites/catch.md) stall in Catch, in a drawer of things Mabb
took in trade and has never once tried to sell.

**Worth:** Two silver as cord. Not for sale, though — Mabb has turned down offers for it twice and given no reason.

A Catch tailor named Ren Ashworth ties eleventh knots into every measuring-cord he cuts for a betrothal, one
knot per year he expects the marriage to hold before either party wants out — the wrong-spaced one is his
private mark for "past counting." He made this cord for [Tallow Ashe's](../geography/sites/catch.md) own wedding,
years before she rang a single Turn early. Whether the marriage it measured is still standing is not written
anywhere yet.
