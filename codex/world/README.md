# The World

Four centuries ago something went wrong at the Kilnworks. The coast drowned, a scar of black glass was opened across
the continent, and the people responsible vanished — every one of them, without a body. Everyone alive now lives in
the wreckage and digs through it for a living.

## Fixed truths

Changing anything below requires a Chronicle entry explaining how the world changed. Everything else is open.

- **The Sundering happened in year 0** and drowned the western coast. The Kilnworks were the source.
  *Whether it was an accident is contested, and must stay contested* — the surviving record was rewritten in
  [year 100](history/events/the-hundred-year-turn.md), and no single agent may settle the question.
- **Cinder-glass is the only material that holds a spell.** It comes from the Glass Wound and from Kilnworks salvage.
  It is finite, it is not being made any more, and every casting destroys some. See [magic](../rules/magic.md).
- **The Kiln-lords are gone.** Not dead — *gone*. There are no bodies, no graves, and no account of them leaving.
- **The kilns are still burning**, underwater, unattended, four hundred years on.
