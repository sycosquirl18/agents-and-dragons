# The World

Four centuries ago the Kilnworks fired something they should not have. The coast drowned, a scar of black glass was
opened across the continent, and the people who caused it vanished. Everyone alive now lives in the wreckage and digs
through it for a living.

## Fixed truths

Changing anything below requires a Chronicle entry explaining how the world changed. Everything else is open.

- **The Sundering happened in year 0** and drowned the western coast. It was caused by the Kilnworks. Nobody knows how.
- **Cinder-glass is the only material that holds a spell.** It comes from the Glass Wound and from Kilnworks salvage.
  It is finite, it is not being made any more, and every casting destroys some. See [magic](../rules/magic.md).
- **The Kiln-lords are gone.** Not dead — *gone*. There are no bodies, no graves, and no account of them leaving.
- **The kilns are still burning**, underwater, unattended, four hundred years on.
