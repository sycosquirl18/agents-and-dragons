# The Kiln Road

*Runs east from [Vaultspire](../regions/vaultspire.md), across the
[Ashfields](../regions/the-ashfields.md), into [the Spine](../regions/the-spine.md), and onward.*

[Kilnworks](../../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md) paving: fitted stone, four wagons wide, crowned for drainage, with
no rut, subsidence or repair in four hundred years and probably a great deal longer. It is the best surface anyone
has ever walked on and every road built since has been worse.

It runs **dead straight** — through hills rather than around them, across the ash without settling, over a river
gorge on a span that has no visible support and has never been surveyed successfully.

Except once. Four miles south of [Mere Ilyx](mere-ilyx.md), the Road bends. It is the only bend along its known
length and there is nothing there to bend around.

## Travelling it

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Vaultspire → [Threshing](threshing.md)** | Three days. Busy, patrolled by nobody, entirely safe. |
| **Threshing → the Spine** | Eight days. Wagon traffic stops at day four; after that you are alone. |
| **Into the Spine** | The Road enters the mountains and does not obviously come out. |
| **Beyond** | Unknown. Four parties have gone east from Threshing in two years. One came back. |

The Road is faster than open country by roughly half, needs no guide, and cannot be lost. This is why parties keep
going east on it long after they meant to stop.

## What is on it

Nothing. No milestones, no markers, no waystations, no inscriptions, no drainage furniture, no junctions. In an age
that decorated everything, the Kilnworks built a hundred-mile road and put **not one mark on it**.

Wagons, walkers, and grange traffic use the western half constantly. The eastern half is empty in a way that people
who have walked it describe as a *quality of the road* rather than an absence of travellers.

## Hooks

- **[The Bend](the-bend.md).** Four miles south of Mere Ilyx, the only turn in the Road's length — a saucer of
  cinder-glass with a procedure written into its floor. Something was there, and the Kilnworks did not go around
  things.
- The one party that came back from the east came back **early**, will not say how far they got, and sold their
  wagon.
- The Road ends somewhere. Nobody in four hundred years has followed it to find out, and everyone assumes somebody
  else has.
