# Catch

*The [Saltmarch](../regions/the-saltmarch.md). About nine hundred people, none of whom sleep at ground level.*

A town built twice: once on the seabed at ebb, once in the air at flood. The lower Catch is a grid of salt-stained
stone lanes, market floor and drying racks that spends half its life underwater. The upper Catch is a lattice of
stilt-houses, walkways and counterweighted platforms slung fifteen feet above it, and the two are connected by
about four hundred rope ladders that are hauled up at the Turn.

Everything here is on a pulley. It is the most ingenious place in the known world and it is poor.

## The market

Catch's market runs for **two hours before the Turn**, which is when the ebb salvage comes in and the
[Vaultspire](../regions/vaultspire.md) buyers must leave or be stranded. The compressed schedule is deliberate:
a buyer with fifty minutes to make the road does not haggle well.

It is the only market in reach that the [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) does not license, because
farmhouse salvage is not vault salvage. Prices run [a third under Vaultspire's](../../../rules/economy.md), and
the goods are legal, and every year somebody in the Cant proposes fixing this.

## People

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Tallow Ashe** | Tide-caller. Rings the Turn. Has rung it four minutes early all year and has told nobody why. |
| **Mabb Orrin** | Buys anything, asks nothing, and is the fence half of Vaultspire's unlicensed salvage goes through — including [Brannoc's](../../../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md). |
| **The Widow Sarn** | Owns the ladders. Not a figure of speech: she owns them, and access to the upper town is hers to withdraw. |

## Dangers

- **Missing the Turn.** The ladders come up whether or not you are on one.
- **Owing the Widow.** She does not threaten and does not need to.
- Being a stranger with clean boots, which reads as Concord.

## Hooks

- **The four minutes.** Tallow rings early because the water arrives early, and she has worked out that it has been
  creeping for a year. She has not reported it because Catch's exemption depends on Catch being uninteresting.
- Mabb has been offered a great deal of money to stop buying from unlicensed divers. Not by the Concord.
