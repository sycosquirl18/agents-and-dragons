# The Drowned Kiln

The original furnace of the kiln-vault that [Vaultspire](../regions/vaultspire.md) is built into. It has been
underwater since year 0 and it is still firing. Everything else in this world is wreckage; the Kiln is the one piece
of the Kilnworks that never stopped working, and nobody has ever seen the part of it that burns.

For what is happening there **right now**, see [The Kiln Still Burns](../../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md).

## Getting in

Only at neap tide, and only from the Underspire. Spring tides shut it completely for eleven days at a stretch.

| Layer | |
| --- | --- |
| **The outer sluice** | Flooded intake channel, sixty feet down. Open roughly forty minutes either side of low neap. Held breath and a rope, unless you have [Drawn Breath](../../../rules/spells/drawn-breath.md). |
| **The outer grate** | Concord seal. Currently cut — see the [Chronicle](../../../chronicle/412-214-the-seal-was-cut.md). |
| **The settling galleries** | Where licensed crews actually worked. Wide, silted, warm. This is the part that appears on charts. |
| **The inner grate** | Kilnworks-made, closed since year 0. No hinge, no lock anyone recognises, no record of it ever being opened. |
| **The firing floor** | Below the inner grate. The heat comes from here and so does the light. Nobody living has been. |

The whole descent is a **six-hour** proposition from slack water. That number is not negotiable and everyone who
works the Kiln plans around it, which is why "the tide turned" is a Tideline euphemism for a funeral.

## The heat

The water in the sluice is blood-warm sixty feet down in late autumn, and this season it is the warmest anyone has
measured. It is warmer nearer the inner grate, which is the expected direction, and it is *also* warmer forty miles
away at [the Weeping Bore](the-weeping-bore.md), which is not.

The Kiln stopped for eleven hours in year 380 and nobody knows why — [the Quiet](../../history/events/the-quiet.md).
It is the only interruption on record.

## Dangers

- **The tide**, which is the real one. Grace, DC 13 to beat a rising sluice, +2 per round once it turns.
- **No air.** Every scene down here runs against breath as well as the clock.
- **[Kiln-wights](../../bestiary/kiln-wight.md)** in the flooded galleries, going about work that ended four
  hundred years ago.
- **The Concord**, which has closed the site and does not need a court to enforce that.

## Hooks

- **The inner grate has no mechanism**, and the seal on the outer grate was cut from the inside. Both of those
  facts are about something being *in there*.
- The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) has bought the salvage rights to the surrounding seabed
  for four years and has never dived a foot of it.
- Old Pellick's charts of the settling galleries disagree with the Concord's, and Pellick's are older. `stub`
- If the Kiln is still firing, something is still feeding it. Nobody has proposed a candidate out loud.
