# The Settling Galleries

The name is a Concord misreading of the original chart — the Kilnworks word is closer to *sorting-floor*.
These are not rooms where silt settled; they are the grading and routing levels of the kiln-vault, six
flooded chambers where raw material was measured, weighed, and directed to the correct furnace below.
Nobody corrected the mistranslation, and the wrong name is now on every licensed chart.

The chambers are wide enough to swim in pairs, warm, and faintly lit by heat-glow rising from the inner grate
below. Silt covers everything, but the sorting channels are still intact under it. The corridors between
chambers get narrower the deeper you descend — shoulder-width by the fourth gallery, chest-width at the
fifth, and the final approach to the inner grate is a squeeze.

Someone has cut new handholds into the passage walls at that last stretch, recently, with proper tools.

## Who is there

| Name | Want |
| --- | --- |
| **Three kiln-wights** | To finish sorting a load that has no destination — they carry baskets of cinder-glass back and forth between chambers, waiting for a handoff that is four hundred years late |
| **[Old Pellick](../../../characters/old-pellick/sheet.md)** | To find Sella's crew before he has to admit they are gone — he says he will not go back, and here he is |
| **The third crew's rope** | Still tethered to a sorting-channel wall anchor. One end goes down. The other is frayed, not cut |

Old Pellick is not supposed to be here. He will not say why he came back. He knows more about the inner grate
than he has admitted to anyone.

## Dangers

- **Kiln-wights** (see [bestiary](../../bestiary/kiln-wight.md)) — they ignore you unless you block the route.
  Get between a wight and its handoff point and it will move you. Wits DC 14 to read the route; Heart DC 13
  to slot into it and become *part* of the job.
- **The silt** — disturbed, it blocks sight entirely. Grace DC 11 to move quickly without raising a cloud;
  disadvantage to all checks while a cloud is up.
- **No air.** Every exchange burns breath. Anyone without
  [Drawn Breath](../../../rules/spells/drawn-breath.md) is on the clock from the moment they leave the sluice.
- **The narrowing.** The fifth-gallery squeeze requires gear to be removed — pack or weapon, one or the other,
  not both. Might DC 11 to force the passage while encumbered; failure means stuck, not through.

## What is hidden

The second gallery has a false wall behind the largest sorting channel — Wits DC 16 to notice the seam, or DC 11
if a wight has just passed through it. Behind the wall: a dry pocket of air, a Kilnworks lamp still burning
on sealed oil, and a ledger in the notation nobody can read. The ledger is dated year 0 — the day of the Sundering.

## Hooks

- **[Old Pellick](../../../characters/old-pellick/sheet.md)** is here and has no explanation. What he knows
  about the inner grate will surface if he is pressed — or if the rope pulls taut.
- **[The frayed rope](../../../quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md)** of the third crew goes down past the inner grate. The rope did not break; it was cut from below, then frayed to look otherwise.
- **[The notation ledger](../../../world/history/eras/the-kilnworks.md)** in the hidden pocket is the oldest
  object in the site and should not exist — the Kilnworks did not use ledgers. They used the notation because
  it did not need them.
