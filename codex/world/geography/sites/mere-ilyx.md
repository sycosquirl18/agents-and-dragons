# Mere Ilyx

*The [Ashfields](../regions/the-ashfields.md). A lake about two miles across. The only standing water in the region.*

Flat country, two feet of ash, no rivers — and then a round lake, deep, cold, and clear enough to see forty feet
down. The ash stops eleven yards from the shoreline in every direction, in a clean ring, as though it were laid
around the mere rather than over it.

Which would mean the mere was already here in year 0, and the [Sundering](../../history/eras/the-sundering.md)'s
ashfall arrived and went around it.

## The shore

Nobody farms the ring, in a region that farms everything. The granges give practical reasons — the ground is bad,
the water is cold, the yield is poor — and the reasons are true and are not why.

What is actually true is that **the mere is loud at night**: a slow knocking, from below, irregular, audible from
the shore and not from a boat. It has been recorded continuously for four centuries. It is not seasonal, it does
not respond to anything, and it has never once been explained.

Grange children are told it is stones settling. Grange adults do not sleep within a mile of it.

## In the water

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Depth** | Not sounded. Lines run out past three hundred feet. |
| **Cold** | Painfully, immediately cold in all seasons — which the warm [Wound](../regions/the-glass-wound.md) forty miles west makes odd rather than notable. |
| **Fish** | Yes, and they are ordinary, and the granges eat them without comment. |
| **The floor** | Visible for the first forty feet: pale, flat, and *regular*. Swimmers describe it as paved. |

## Hooks

- **Paved.** Diving it is Might DC 15 for the cold alone, and no salvor from Vaultspire has ever been invited.
  [Brannoc](../../../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) could do it and would want to know why nobody has.
- The knocking is **from below the floor**, not from the water.
- The ash ring means something was here before year 0. The
  [Kilnworks](../../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md) built nothing this far east except
  [the Road](the-kiln-road.md) — which passes four miles south of the mere and, uniquely along its length,
  **bends** to do it.
