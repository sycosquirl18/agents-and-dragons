# Harrow Grange

*[The Ashfields](../regions/the-ashfields.md), half a day off the Kiln Road, north of [Mere Ilyx](mere-ilyx.md).*

The fourth grange, and the one that still owes [Gault Marrow](../../characters/gault-marrow/sheet.md) his winnings.
Smaller than Threshing's eleven, walled the same as any other since the
[Reaving](../../history/eras/the-reaving.md), and known regionally for one thing: Harrow does not sell its grain
at the season's price. It sells it early, at a **discount**, to the other ten granges, who pay for the certainty
of a fixed number before the Long Room even sits. The other factors call it Harrow's premium — the price Harrow
takes for carrying everyone else's risk of a bad season.

It has worked for six generations. This is the first year Harrow has taken the deal and not paid out to anyone,
because for the first time nobody has asked to buy the certainty. Every other grange thinks it already knows what
the season will do.

## Who is there

| Name | Want |
| --- | --- |
| **Reeve Sarn Harrow** | Head of the grange, keeper of the premium book. Wants the debt to [Gault Marrow](../../characters/gault-marrow/sheet.md) paid quietly, off the books, before anyone at the Long Room asks why Harrow is suddenly short of coin rather than long on it. |
| **Tam Ossory** | Harrow's lye-master, runs the ash pits at the grange's east wall. Has been told to slow the alkali works for the season and does not know why — the pits are Harrow's second income and idling them costs more than the grain debt does. |

## What is dangerous

- **Grange law**, as everywhere in the Ashfields — Reeve Sarn is the only court on the property, and a debt she
  doesn't want raised in public is a debt she can make disappear along with whoever raised it, if pushed carelessly.
- **The ash pits.** Lye leached from Harrow's burn-ash for soap and tanning trade; wading in without oilcloth is a
  Might DC 12 save or a chemical burn bad enough to end a scene early.

## What is hidden

Harrow slowed the lye works the same week it quietly told the Long Room it would not renew the premium for next
season — the two decisions were made an hour apart, by Reeve Sarn alone, and she has told nobody why. Wits DC 15
against Reeve Sarn's own ledger (kept in the granary office, not the strongbox) turns up that the ash pits'
takings were already spent, in full, on a single unlogged payment the week before the grain went unsold. `stub`
