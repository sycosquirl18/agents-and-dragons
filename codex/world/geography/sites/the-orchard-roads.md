# The Orchard Roads

*The [Saltmarch](../regions/the-saltmarch.md). Twenty-odd miles of drowned lanes; land six hours a day.*

Before year 0 this was orchard country: walled lanes, farmhouses, wells, boundary stones, a chapel every few miles.
The [Sundering](../../history/eras/the-sundering.md) put it under by about nine feet. At ebb it all comes back —
grey, salt-killed, and standing exactly where it stood — and you can walk the lanes between the dead trees with the
walls still at shoulder height.

It is where every salvor in the region learns the trade. The finds are poor, the rules are simple, and the mistakes
are survivable right up until they are not.

## Working it

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What is there** | Pots, tools, coin, ironwork, doors. Occasionally a sealed cellar, which is a month's wages. |
| **Who may** | Anyone. Unlicensed by definition — the [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md)'s writ is vaults. |
| **The rule** | Turn back at the third bell. Everyone says it. Everyone who ignores it is a story. |

Finding an unopened cellar is Wits DC 13 with a day's search, DC 15 in a lane that has been worked before — and
almost every lane has been worked before, four hundred times.

## Navigating

The lanes are a maze at eye level and obvious from a height, and there is no height. Crews navigate by counting
walls and by the chapel spires, three of which still stand above the flood and serve as the only fixed marks in
twenty miles.

Losing the count with the tide coming is the classic Saltmarch death and it is how the third bell rule got written.

## Hooks

- **The tracks.** Something crosses the lanes at flood, in the same three places, and leaves marks in the silt that
  are there at every ebb. Too heavy for a person and wrong for a [wight](../../bestiary/kiln-wight.md). `stub`
- The **fourth spire** appears on pre-Reaving maps and is not there. The maps are otherwise accurate.
- Cellars sealed *from the inside* turn up about once a decade. The trade's explanation is that people hid from the
  water. The trade does not dwell on how they sealed them.
