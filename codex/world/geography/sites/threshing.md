# Threshing

*The [Ashfields](../regions/the-ashfields.md). Two thousand people, eleven granaries, one road.*

The market town of the grange country and the place where [Vaultspire](../regions/vaultspire.md)'s bread is
actually priced. It sits where the [Kiln Road](the-kiln-road.md) crosses the wagon route north, which is the whole
reason it exists and the whole reason it matters.

Low, walled, and built of fired ash-brick the colour of bone. Nothing here is older than the
[Reaving](../../history/eras/the-reaving.md), because the Reaving burned whatever was.

## The Long Room

Grain price for the entire region is set in one room above the north granary, by eleven grange factors, over two
days each autumn. The [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md)'s buyers are not admitted and are informed of the
result.

Vaultspire regards this as a cartel. The [Granges](../../factions/ashfield-granges.md) regard it as the only reason
eleven farms are not underbid into ruin by four buyers, and point out that they learned the technique from the
[Glass Peace](../../history/events/the-glass-peace.md).

## Dangers

Threshing is safe, which is the danger — it is the last safe place before the Road, and heroes overstock, oversleep,
and overtalk here. Grange law applies: the factors judge, and their judgements are commercial.

## Hooks

- **Three granges have stopped selling entirely.** Cassardy will not say why and is not bluffing.
- [Wick Sorrel](../people/wick-sorrel.md) has noticed two of them have stopped selling wool too — a second
  hoarding pattern nobody at the Long Room has connected to the grain yet.
- [Ynda Cray](../people/ynda-cray.md) has spotted a third: the same two granges cleared every wagon-yard debt
  early this season. Nobody has put all three signals side by side.
- [Corda Vane](../people/corda-vane.md) has bitten into the stored grain itself: it's three years old, dressed as
  new, in the same two granaries.
- The six pre-year-100 documents are held *here*, in the Long Room's strongbox, and the Covenant's fourth request
  was made by someone who did not ask.
- Hesk has sold wheels to four eastbound parties in two years. **One came back.**
