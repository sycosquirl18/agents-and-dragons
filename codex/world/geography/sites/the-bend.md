# The Bend

*[The Kiln Road](the-kiln-road.md), four miles south of [Mere Ilyx](mere-ilyx.md). The only turn in a hundred
miles of dead-straight paving.*

The Road curves around a shallow saucer of ground perhaps sixty feet across, no deeper than a cellar, floored
entirely in cinder-glass gone smooth and slightly domed, like something poured and left to cool before anyone
thought to level it. From directly above or straight on, the floor reads as nothing — glare, and a faint grey
marbling that looks accidental. Stand at the rim and squint along it edge-on, low, at the marbling, and it
resolves: a single unbroken line of [Kilnworks notation](../../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md#the-notation-problem),
one continuous mark laid into the glass itself, following the whole curve of the saucer. It is the only notation
anyone has found that was never meant to be read from above.

Nothing else marks the site. No wall, no marker, no ash-fall difference from the surrounding Ashfields — which is
itself strange, since the Kilnworks built nothing defensive and marked nothing, and yet went to the trouble of
routing four hundred miles of dead-straight road *around* this one thing rather than through it.

## Who is there

| Name | |
| --- | --- |
| **Wend Iscarrow** | Ashfield grange-scout, works the empty stretch between Threshing and the Spine buying up strayed grange stock. Has crossed the Bend nine times and refuses a tenth without being paid for it. Wants someone else to go first. |
| **Cray Osten** | A Covenant surveyor, alone, camped at the rim under a grange traveling-permit that names a different destination. Has been squinting at the glass for six days and has stopped writing anything down. Wants the line read before anyone from Vaultspire arrives to claim the site. |

## What is dangerous

- **The dome cracks underweight**, not underfoot — a cart or three people standing close together will do it, one
  person walking will not. Grace DC 13 to get clear once it starts to give; failure drops you through to a
  gallery of unknown depth beneath the glass.
- **Glass-crakes** ([bestiary](../../bestiary/glass-crake.md)) nest under the dome's overhang on the low side and
  scatter, shrieking, if the glass is walked on near dawn or dusk — harmless, but loud enough to be heard from the
  Road, which is how Wend knows the count of everyone who has ever tried the crossing.

## What is hidden

The notation line is not a sentence — it is a **procedure**, read start to finish only by walking the rim at the
correct squinting angle while it is being read aloud, one mark taking roughly a breath. Wits DC 18 (or DC 15 with
Cray's six days of notes) to find the correct start point and angle. What it describes has never been carried out:
nobody has read past the ninth mark of roughly forty before losing the angle, tripping the glare, or reaching the
crack line. Whether finishing it does anything is unknown by design — the Road bending around this one unmarked
thing, in an age that marked nothing, is the whole of the argument for why it might.

## Hooks

- **What is under the dome.** The crack drops into
  [the gallery below](the-bend-gallery.md) — someone still walks its walls, cutting.
- **Cray Osten's six days of notes** — if the party doesn't read the line itself, his notes are the shortcut, and
  the [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) will want them back.
- Wend has been paid, in the past, by someone in Threshing to report anyone else who tries the crossing. He has
  never been told why, or by whom.
