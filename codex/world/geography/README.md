# Geography

Nobody knows the shape of the world. What is known is a stretch of drowned coast, the scar behind it, the dry
country behind that, and rumours in three directions.

Everything is oriented on the [Glass Wound](regions/the-glass-wound.md), because the Wound runs dead straight from
the sea into the interior and is visible from almost everywhere. "Wound-ward" means toward it. It is the only
landmark this world agrees on.

## The known world

Ordered from the water inland — which is also the order in which the
[Sundering](../history/eras/the-sundering.md) ruined them.

| Region | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Saltmarch](regions/the-saltmarch.md) | drowned coast | A hundred miles of tidal ruin. Land for six hours a day. |
| [Vaultspire](regions/vaultspire.md) | city | Salvage city inside a half-sunk kiln-vault. The party's home port. |
| [The Glass Wound](regions/the-glass-wound.md) | scar | The trench the Sundering opened. Every piece of cinder-glass comes from here. |
| [The Ashfields](regions/the-ashfields.md) | farmland | Dry, flat, extraordinarily fertile. Feeds Vaultspire and resents it. |

Named places within regions are in [sites/](sites/README.md).

## The unknown world

treat them as genuinely open.

## Distance and travel

**Distances are counted in tides, not miles.** The Saltmarch is passable only at low water, so a journey's length
depends on when you leave, and being wrong about that is the commonest way to die in this part of the world.

Overland routes through the Ashfields ignore the moon but are slower, and the
[Kiln Road](sites/the-kiln-road.md) is faster than both for as far as it goes your way.

Travel and encounter rules are **not yet written** — see the missing-rules list in
[`rules/README.md`](../../rules/README.md).

## Adding to the map

Regions are large enough to be worth a file. Anything smaller — a town, a cave system, a lake, a ruin — is a
**site**, and goes in [sites/](sites/README.md) linked from its region. When a region accumulates more sites than
its file can index cleanly, split it into its own directory rather than letting either file grow.
