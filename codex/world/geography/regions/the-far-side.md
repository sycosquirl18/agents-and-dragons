# The Far Side

The country on the other side of the [Glass Wound](the-glass-wound.md). Three miles away. Nobody has stood on it in
four hundred years.

It is not hostile, hidden, or hard to see — on a clear day from the rim you can make out scrub, low hills, and what
are probably trees. It is simply on the far side of a bottomless trench that nothing will bridge, and the coast
route around the Wound's mouth is open sea.

Two things are known and are not discussed together:

- There is **no smoke** over there, ever, and there never has been.
- The Wound is [getting wider](the-glass-wound.md) at both edges, so the Far Side is not merely unreached. It is
  receding.
