# The Farshore

Land is visible west from the top of [Vaultspire](vaultspire.md) on a clear day: a grey line that does not move,
about thirty miles out across water the [Sundering](../../history/eras/the-sundering.md) made.

Boats go. Roughly two thirds come back, which is a good enough ratio that the crossing is a trade rather than an
adventure, and a bad enough one that nobody does it twice for pleasure.

The ones that return bring back timber, dried fish, and consistent accounts of a coast with **nobody on it** —
no smoke, no boats, no lights. They also come back on average a day and a half later than the passage should take,
and none of the crews can say where the time went.
