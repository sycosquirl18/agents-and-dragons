# The Glass Wound

The scar. A trench of black glass running inland from the drowned coast, dead straight, for a distance nobody has
honestly measured — the usual claim is ninety miles and the usual claim is a guess.

All [cinder-glass](../../../rules/magic.md) comes from here. This makes the Wound the most valuable ground in the
world and the least pleasant to be near.

## The trench

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Width** | Three miles at the coast, narrowing inland. Slightly wider every year — about a hand's breadth, measurably. |
| **Depth** | Unknown. Lines have been run out to nine hundred feet without touching anything. |
| **Temperature** | Warm to the touch, year-round, in all weather. Warmer as you descend. |
| **Sound** | None. It is conspicuously, unpleasantly quiet down there; the walls do not echo. |
| **Straightness** | Absolute. It ignores rock, river and valley, which no fault does. |

Nobody living has been to the bottom. The [cutting works](../sites/the-weeping-bore.md) go down about two hundred
feet, and that is the floor of the trade, not a threshold — glass improves the whole way down. Chip above sixty
feet, poor [Plate](../../../rules/magic.md#grades-of-glass) from sixty, sound Plate below a hundred and forty. The
Bore's [level table](../sites/the-weeping-bore.md#the-works) is the authority on which depth yields what.

## The glass

Cinder-glass is *cut* from the Wound's walls, not mined from a seam — the whole trench is the same substance the
whole way down, and quality is purely a function of depth. Deeper glass is clearer, castable at a lower DC, and
harder to bring up alive.

The [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) licenses cutting exactly as it licenses diving, and for the same
reason: it is the only way to keep the price where it is.

**Ruling (Arbiter, 412).** The trench being bottomless does not make glass renewable, and the
[magic rules](../../../rules/magic.md) are right that the supply is shrinking. What is finite is not the substance
but the **face** — glass has to be cut off a worked wall by a crew that can be got back up, and every foot cut makes
the next foot deeper, hotter and slower. Cut faces do not heal, and the widening adds rim, not reachable wall. The
world is not running out of cinder-glass. It is running out of cinder-glass it can reach.

## Crossing it

You do not. Three miles of vertical glass with no bottom is a border, not an obstacle, and everything on
[the far side](the-far-side.md) has been out of reach for four hundred years.

Twice, bridges have been started. Both attempts ended when the anchors would not hold in the glass — it is not that
the glass is brittle, it is that **nothing bonds to it**, including itself.

## Dangers

- **[Glass-crakes](../../bestiary/glass-crake.md)**, which are not dangerous and will cost you your cut anyway.
- **Cutting spall.** The commonest injury in the world after drowning.
- **The warmth.** Crews working below a hundred feet come up cheerful, talkative and unable to account for an hour
  or two. It passes. Everybody says it passes.

## Hooks

- **The widening.** A hand's breadth a year, for four hundred years, is a mile. Someone has the old surveys.
- **Why nothing bonds to it**, which is a question about what the Wound is rather than what it produces.
- The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) sends people to walk the rim and does not send them to cut.
