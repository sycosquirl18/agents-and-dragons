# The Ashfields

Dry, flat, and the only place in reach that grows enough grain to feed [Vaultspire](vaultspire.md). The soil is ash
laid down in year 0 and it is extraordinarily good, which the Ashfielders consider the one thing the Sundering owed
them.

They sell to Vaultspire at Vaultspire's prices and have opinions about it.
