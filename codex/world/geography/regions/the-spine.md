# The Spine

Mountains east of the [Ashfields](the-ashfields.md), eight days out along the
[Kiln Road](../sites/the-kiln-road.md), which runs into them and does not obviously come out.

The Kilnworks quarried here — the workings are visible from the Ashfields on a clear morning, terraced into the
western faces at a scale that makes the [vaults](../../history/eras/the-kilnworks.md) look modest. Then they
stopped, apparently abruptly, and there is unworked stone still sitting on the cutting floors.

Nobody in Vaultspire has any commercial reason to go. Everything the Spine has, the Wound has closer.
