# The Saltmarch

A hundred miles of coast that used to be dry. The [Sundering](../../history/eras/the-sundering.md) put it under, but
only just — at low water the old roads, walls and orchards come back, salt-dead and walkable, and at high water they
are gone again.

**The Saltmarch is a place that exists for six hours a day.** Everything about it follows from that.

## Living on a schedule

Every settlement here is built for the two hours before the water and the two hours after. Marches — the local word
for a working shift — run to the tide table, not the sun, so a Saltmarcher's day slips forward fifty minutes
against a Vaultspire clerk's and neither of them finds this remarkable.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Ebb** | Six hours of land. Farming, salvage, travel, burial, everything. |
| **The Turn** | Forty minutes of warning. The water does not rush; it *arrives*, everywhere at once, from below. |
| **Flood** | Six hours of sea. You are on stilts, on a boat, on high ground, or you are dead. |

Being caught out is Grace [DC 13, rising by 2 each exchange](../../../rules/checks.md#difficulty). Nobody
experienced gets caught out. It kills visitors and the newly confident.

## Why anyone stays

The Saltmarch grows **saltwort and marsh-rice**, which nothing else in the region does, and it holds four hundred
years of ordinary domestic salvage — the pots, tools, coin and doors of a normal drowned country. It is poor
salvage and it is *unlicensed*, because the [Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md)'s writ covers vaults and not
farmhouses.

That exemption is the Saltmarch's whole economy and the Concord has been reconsidering it for two years.

## Dangers

- **The Turn**, above. It is not a metaphor and it is not survivable by being brave.
- **Reading a borrowed tide table.** Tables are local to within a few miles. Vaultspire's table will kill you at Catch.
- **Marchers who have decided you are a Concord surveyor**, which currently everyone is assumed to be.
