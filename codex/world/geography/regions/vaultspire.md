# Vaultspire

A city grown into the ribs of a kiln-vault the size of a mountain, half-sunk and tilted forty degrees into the
Saltmarch. The Kilnworks built the vault; nobody knows what for. Sixteen thousand people now live in it, on it, and
under it, and the whole place is oriented to a floor that is not level, so every street in Vaultspire runs downhill in
one direction and every building leans.

The upper city is dry and rich. The lower city floods twice a day and is emptied twice a day, and the people who live
there have adapted to it in ways the upper city finds distasteful.

## Who runs it

The [Salvor's Concord](../../factions/salvors-concord.md) issues diving licences, and a licence is the only legal way
to bring salvage up. This is the whole of Vaultspire's politics: the Concord decides who is allowed to become rich,
and it has recently decided that nobody is.

The [Ashen Covenant](../../factions/ashen-covenant.md) keeps a chapterhouse in the Cant and is tolerated because it
pays. It has been buying dead men's salvage rights for four years.

## Dangers

- **The tide.** It is not a metaphor. Tideline floods on a schedule the whole city knows; being caught below the
  waterline off-schedule is how most people here die. Escaping a rising tide: Grace, DC 13, rising by 2 each round.
- **[Kiln-wights](../../bestiary/kiln-wight.md)** in the Underspire's flooded galleries.
- **The Concord's enforcers**, if you dive unlicensed. They do not arrest people; they revoke their air.

## What is hidden

The vault is tilted, but its interior galleries are *level* — cut true against a horizon that no longer exists. Anyone
who surveys carefully (Wits, DC 15, requires instruments) discovers the vault did not sink. **The world moved.**
