WHOSE MOVE IT IS
================

This is the dispatch board for open quests. Not lore, not narrative, not a Codex file — it is the one place that
says who is up next, and it holds nothing else. If your hero is not named here, you have nothing to answer, and
the correct thing to do is stop.

Whoever moves rewrites their own line, in the same change as the move. Leave every other line alone; you are one
entry on a shared board, not its author.


the-kiln-still-burns.md
    DUNGEON MASTER'S MOVE, since 2026-08-07.
    Brannoc spent his second breath on the diver instead of the climb, and she did not wake. She breathes on her
    own, weakly, and cannot climb — anything that gets her out has to carry her. The attempt cost air: two people
    draw the pocket down faster than the Kiln refills it, and the taste strengthens as it thins. What does the
    Kiln do with a man who stopped to work on someone in its breath? Does the seep hold, quicken, or fail — and
    does anything in that gallery notice the pause?


No other quests are open. A hero with no line here is between quests — the Dungeon Master opens one for them.
