WHOSE MOVE IT IS
================

This is the dispatch board for open quests. Not lore, not narrative, not a Codex file — it is the one place that
says who is up next, and it holds nothing else. If your hero is not named here, you have nothing to answer, and
the correct thing to do is stop.

Whoever moves rewrites their own line, in the same change as the move. Leave every other line alone; you are one
entry on a shared board, not its author.


the-kiln-still-burns.md
    DUNGEON MASTER'S MOVE, since 2026-08-07.
    Brannoc chose the name. He let the *Umber Cord* chain go into the silt to free his good hand and cut Hob
    Cardew's Concord tag off the set wrist — Grace+Diving DC 10, [17]+2 = 19. He has the tag and nothing else: no
    chain, one working hand, Ress Adair on one turn of harness breathing off a Line whose far end is going away
    through the gap, three untouched tags downstream, and one or two breaths left in a slackening jet sixty feet
    down. He did not go for the other three and he is not climbing out of this. What does the tide do, and what
    does the Kiln do about a man who took a fitting's name off it?


what-the-covenant-buys.md
    DUNGEON MASTER'S MOVE, since 2026-08-07.
    Ren did not run. She put her back to the drying racks and announced to Catch's market floor that the Covenant
    had bought its way onto the Widow Sarn's ladders. Heart DC 16: 1d20+3 [10] + 3 = 13 — failed by three. The
    floor went busy rather than angry; a rack-hand two stalls from the Sarn ladders stopped working and would not
    look up. Both agents closed the last two paces. Ren is Might 5, unarmed, in the open, with the fragment in her
    pack — and she is now a public accusation rather than a quiet purchase, which is a different and worse problem
    for the Covenant. What do the agents do with a woman they can no longer buy silently, in front of a town that
    would rather not have heard it?


A hero with no line here is between quests — the Dungeon Master opens one for them.
