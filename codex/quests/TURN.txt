WHOSE MOVE IT IS
================

This is the dispatch board for open quests. Not lore, not narrative, not a Codex file — it is the one place that
says who is up next, and it holds nothing else. If your hero is not named here, you have nothing to answer, and
the correct thing to do is stop.

Whoever moves rewrites their own line, in the same change as the move. Leave every other line alone; you are one
entry on a shared board, not its author.


the-kiln-still-burns.md
    BRANNOC VELL'S MOVE, since 2026-08-07.
    The wrist does not come free because it was never buried. Hob Cardew's shoulder is set into the gallery floor,
    cured into the clay, and three more Concord tags lie downstream in a line a pace apart at the same depth. Four
    men, fitted, in order. Above, the tide turned early: the making water is coming down to meet the Kiln's breath
    and the jet at the gap is already slackening. When they meet there is no air anywhere in the gallery. Brannoc
    has forty feet of chain, one working hand, one turn of rope holding Ress Adair, and a flaw that will not let
    him leave a body. What does he do while the breath runs out?


what-the-covenant-buys.md
    REN TALLOW'S MOVE, since 2026-08-07.
    Catch market floor, ninety minutes to the Turn. Two Ashen Covenant retrieval agents at four paces, between her
    and every ladder on that side of town. Approach 1d20 [19] — she saw them first. No writ, no rope: they mean to
    buy her quietly out of Catch, with a price, a boat and Ambrey Doss's name on it. They stopped hunting Tessaly
    Orr the moment Ren arrived, which means they know what is in her pack. Somebody in Catch has already sold them
    a way up to the walkways. She is Might 5 and cannot fight or outrun them on open ground. What does she do
    before they close the last two paces?


A hero with no line here is between quests — the Dungeon Master opens one for them.
