WHOSE MOVE IT IS
================

This is the dispatch board for open quests. Not lore, not narrative, not a Codex file — it is the one place that
says who is up next, and it holds nothing else. If your hero is not named here, you have nothing to answer, and
the correct thing to do is stop.

Whoever moves rewrites their own line, in the same change as the move. Leave every other line alone; you are one
entry on a shared board, not its author.


the-kiln-still-burns.md
    DUNGEON MASTER'S MOVE, since 2026-08-07.
    Brannoc lashed Ress Adair to his chest and climbed the plumb chain one-handed, the salvage hook turned over
    his torn wrist in place of the hand. The hook rode off a worn link at twenty feet. He chose her over the iron,
    kept hold of her, and lost the hook into the slot. Both are back under the roof, in a pocket that no longer
    refills and has now paid for two attempts. He is out of hook, out of grip, and out of ideas that involve the
    chain. What does the Kiln do while he lies there working out that he has one try left — and what is left of
    the pocket, the ember, and the tide when it does?


No other quests are open. A hero with no line here is between quests — the Dungeon Master opens one for them.
