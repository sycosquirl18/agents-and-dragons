# What the Covenant Buys

**Hook:** The [Ashen Covenant](../world/factions/ashen-covenant.md)'s retrieval agents have been in
[Catch](../world/geography/sites/catch.md) since Day 214 looking for [Tessaly Orr](../characters/tessaly-orr/sheet.md).
They have not found her. On Day 215 they stopped looking, because
[Ren Tallow](../characters/ren-tallow/sheet.md) walked into the same town carrying the fragment that got her out of
the Cant, and they would rather have the document than the glassmaker.

## Objectives

- [ ] Get off the market floor before the Turn
- [ ] Find out who in Catch sold the Covenant its way up
- [ ] Keep the pre-Turn fragment out of Covenant hands
- [ ] Decide what Tessaly Orr is owed, and whether she is told

## Known

- Two Covenant agents are in Catch. They came to the lower town openly, paid, and asked nobody about Tessaly.
- They did not come with a writ. Approach 1d20 [19] — Ren noticed them at four paces, on the market floor, with
  the racks at her back and the ladders behind them.
- Somebody in Catch took Covenant money for access to the upper town. The ladders are the
  [Widow Sarn](../world/geography/sites/catch.md)'s, and access is hers to withdraw.
- Tessaly Orr is in the upper walkways and does not know the hunt has changed shape.
- The Turn is running four minutes early, as it has all year. Catch knows. The Covenant does not.

## Unknown

- What the Covenant means to do with the fragment — bury it again, or use it.
- Whether Ambrey Doss, Ren's old teacher, knows she is here.

## Stakes

If the fragment goes back to the Cant, the contradiction dies with it and the Covenant's account of the
[Hundred-Year Turn](../world/history/events/the-hundred-year-turn.md) stands unchallenged. If Ren runs and Tessaly
is left in the upper town, the agents still have a glassmaker to fall back on and a ladder-owner already paid.
