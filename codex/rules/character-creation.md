# Character Creation

## Stats

Roll `4d6` four times, once per stat, in order: **Might, Grace, Wits, Heart**. Use the
`roll_dice` tool and record the tape on the sheet.

Do not re-roll. Do not swap. A hero with a 5 in Heart is a specific person; a hero with four 13s is nobody.

If the total of all four modifiers is below −2, the hero may raise one stat by 2. That is the only adjustment allowed.

## Health

**HP = 8 + Might modifier.** Minimum 4.

## Skills

Pick **two** skills the hero is trained in. Training is worth **−3 DC** on checks it clearly covers.
Skills are free-form — *diving*, *Kilnworks notation*, *street brawling*, *lying to clerks* — but must be narrow
enough to fail to apply sometimes.

## Trait, bond, flaw

One line each. These are what the [Adventurer](../../.github/workflows/adventurer.md) actually plays.

- **Trait** — how they come across.
- **Bond** — a person, place, or debt they will take a real risk for.
- **Flaw** — the thing that reliably gets them into trouble. It must have teeth. "Too brave" is not a flaw.

## Starting kit

- Clothes, a knife, and a pack.
- One tool set matching a trained skill.
- One weapon, or one piece of armour (Armour DC 12).
- `2d6 × 5` silver. See [economy](economy.md).
- **No cinder-glass.** Heroes do not start with [magic](magic.md); it must be found, bought, or taken.

## Level

There are no levels. Heroes improve by acquiring things, allies, reputation, and knowledge — all of which are
recorded in the Codex and can be taken away again.

Advancement: after a quest resolves, raise one stat by 1 (max 18), or gain one new skill. Recorded on the sheet with
the quest that earned it.
