# Held breath

Half this world's work happens underwater. A diver on held breath gets **four exchanges** of ordinary work — call
it two minutes — with no roll at all. Cold, exertion, or panic cuts that to three.

From the fifth exchange on, the diver rolls **Might** at the start of each one, before whatever else they are
attempting:

| Exchange | DC |
| --- | --- |
| 5th | 10 |
| 6th | 13 |
| 7th | 16 |
| 8th and after | +3 each |

Success buys that exchange. Failure means the breath goes: the diver is **Wounded** with the named injury *drowning*
and gets one more exchange to reach air, a [Drawn Breath](spells/drawn-breath.md) or a line out. Failing that, they
are **Down** — and [Down underwater with nobody on the rope is dead](combat.md#dying).

A hero surfacing on a failed roll is Wounded, not safe. Held breath does not recover in the water: **a full
exchange breathing air resets the count**, nothing less.

Casting, shouting, or taking [harm](combat.md#harm) underwater costs one exchange of air immediately — jump a row
on the table.

Surfacing fast from depth is its own problem and this rule does not cover it. Depth-sickness is a `rules-gap` if it
ever comes up in play.
