# Cold Light

**Effect:** A palm-sized patch of light, bright as a good lamp, fixed to whatever you were touching when it caught.
It burns underwater, in wind, and in the dead air below the sluice, where no flame will stay lit.
**Duration:** Six hours, or until the object it caught on is destroyed.
**Misfire (natural 1):** It catches on *you* — skin, not clothing — and burns for the full six hours. You cannot
put it out, cover it, or dim it, and you are now the brightest thing in the dark and cannot stop being it.

The second spell every crew wants and the first one they can rarely afford. It is why the deep galleries of the
[Underspire](../../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md) are mapped at all.

Note for anyone taking one into the [Weeping Bore](../../world/geography/sites/the-weeping-bore.md): the three-lamp
rule was written about lamps. Whether Cold Light counts has never been tested, and the people who would know are
the people the rule exists to protect.
