# The Held Wall

**Tradition:** [Kilnworks notation](../traditions.md#kilnworks-notation) — the longest unbroken inscription in circulation.
**Load:** 3.
**Effect:** Water stops crossing a line you draw, up to about ten feet wide and as high as it needs to be. It does
not push water back — it simply will not let any more through.
**Duration:** Ten minutes. There is no way to extend it and no warning before it ends.
**Misfire (natural 1):** It holds perfectly, and everything it held arrives at once when it lapses. Ten minutes of
tide in a moment, on whatever side you were standing.

The most expensive spell in common circulation, because in the [Saltmarch](../../world/geography/regions/the-saltmarch.md)
and the flooding half of [Vaultspire](../../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md) it is the difference between a
schedule and a grave.

Ten minutes is not enough to work in. It is enough to **leave**, and every salvor who has ever bought one has been
told that to their face by the seller.
