# The Held Hitch

**Tradition:** [Kilnworks notation](../traditions.md#kilnworks-notation) — a hoist-mark, cut for a foundry winch
taking on an uneven load, not for a diver with a torn hand and someone else's weight on the line.

**Load:** 2 — the failure doesn't just slip, it commits the line to holding wrong.

**Effect:** Cut the mark into a rope, chain, or cable and speak it before you haul. For the duration, the marked
line will not slip or pay out under load on its own — advantage on any Might or Grace check to climb, haul, or
hold that line while it is bearing more than your own weight, including carrying another person.

**Duration:** Ten minutes, or until the line goes slack for a full ten-count, whichever comes first.

**Misfire (natural 1):** The mark reads the load once and locks to that reading — the line seizes at its current
length and will not be paid out, shortened, or unclipped by hand for the duration, no matter what is on it or what
changes. A diver who needed to lower away, cut loose, or take up slack cannot; the line holds exactly what it held
the instant it misfired, and nothing about it can be renegotiated until the ten minutes run out.

Cut for riggers hauling ore up a shaft with a crew below they couldn't see, not for a hand that might need to let
go. A diver comes up with someone else's weight on the line, or the line decides for both of them how far up is
enough.
