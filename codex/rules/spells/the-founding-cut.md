# The Founding Cut

**Tradition:** [Kilnworks notation](../traditions.md#kilnworks-notation) — written for riggers bracing machinery
against a flooding floor, not for people. You can tell it is Kilnworks by how little it cares that you are alive.
**Load:** 3.
**Effect:** Cut the mark into any fixed structure — a wall, a pillar, a kiln housing, a chain's dead-eye. For the
duration, nothing you did not choose can move you more than ten feet from that mark: not surge, not current, not a
grapple, not a collapse. You can still walk, climb, or swim away under your own power at any time.
**Duration:** Ten minutes.
**Misfire (natural 1):** The mark holds you as well as it holds everyone else. For the same ten minutes you also
cannot willingly leave the ten-foot radius — the glass does not distinguish the tide trying to take you from you
trying to go. If the water is still rising when the ten minutes runs out, that is what you were holding against.

Cut for galleries where the tide moves faster than a diver can brace, not for holding a line in a fight. A crew
that has been swept off a chain once buys this before they buy rope.
