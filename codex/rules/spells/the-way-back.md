# The Way Back

**Tradition:** Salvor's marks — cut fast and ugly, a single scratched arrow inside a circle.
**Load:** 2.
**Effect:** Cast it and the arrow inside the glass points, unerringly, toward the nearest breathable air or open
water — through silt, dark, or a flooded gallery with no line to follow. Advantage on the next Grace or Wits check
made to navigate back to that air or water within the scene.
**Duration:** Until you reach it, or ten minutes, whichever is first.
**Misfire (natural 1):** The arrow still points, and it is still confident. It points at the nearest *sealed*
water or dead air instead — a flooded dead end, a chamber with no way out — and you believe it as completely as
you would have believed the truth. You get no check to notice the difference until you have already committed to
following it.

Cut for crews working the settling galleries below the Drowned Kiln, where the silt closes over a line in minutes
and no chart agrees with the last one. It does not tell you what is safe. It tells you which way is out, which is
not always the same thing.
