# Glasskin

**Effect:** The next [harm](../combat.md#harm) that would move you along the ladder does not. The glass takes it
instead and is gone — which it would have been anyway.
**Duration:** Until it takes something, or until the scene ends.
**Misfire (natural 1):** It takes the harm and hands it back inward. You are **Wounded** regardless of what actually
happened to you, with a named injury of the DM's choosing that does not match the fiction and cannot be explained
to a surgeon.

Cast in the moment, not in advance — the inscription reads as present tense, and nobody has made it hold overnight.

It does not make you harder to hit, it does not end a fight, and it does not remove a single roll. It buys one
mistake. Salvors who can afford one describe carrying it as the difference between being careful and being brave,
and are generally clear about which of those keeps you alive.
