# The Witnessed Ground

**Tradition:** Covenant plainscript. Copperplate letters spiral inward instead of running in lines — meant to be
read from the outside once, then never again.

**Load:** 1 — it fails quietly, or not at all.

**Effect:** On casting, the glass records the exact seafloor or ground surface within a thirty-foot radius of the
caster — every ledge, silt line, and buried timber — as it stands at that instant. The shard does not act; it
*holds*. Break it later, anywhere, and it unfolds into a fixed relief no larger than a dinner plate, accurate to
the inch, that cannot be altered, forged, or argued with. The Salvor's Concord accepts a Witnessed Ground as
binding testimony in a boundary or licence dispute, the only kind of evidence it does.

**Duration:** Permanent. The relief does not decay, silt over, or fade.

**Misfire (natural 1):** The shard shatters as normal, but it does not witness the present. It reaches back and
holds the ground as it was at some earlier moment the caster did not choose and will not learn — an hour ago, a
season ago, once as far back as the Turn. Nothing about the false relief looks wrong. Whoever relies on it later
relies on a lie that reads exactly like the truth, and the Concord will not know to doubt it either.
