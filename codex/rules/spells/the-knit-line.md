# The Knit Line

**Tradition:** [Salvor's marks](../traditions.md#salvors-marks) — scratched onto the glass with a wet thumbnail, the
way every diver learns it from the crew before them.
**Load:** 2.
**Effect:** Touch one torn strap, cut rope, split seam, or snapped buckle no wider than your hand. It reassembles
and holds as if whole — full strength, not a patch — for ten minutes.
**Duration:** Ten minutes, then it parts again exactly where it tore.
**Misfire (natural 1):** It reassembles the wrong tear. Whatever you are wearing that is already frayed —
harness, boot, glove — knits itself shut around whatever is nearest to it: your own hand, a line, another diver's
sleeve. Cutting free costs a turn and whatever you cut with.

Every salvor's crew has a story about the season somebody's glove fused to a chain three fathoms down. It is why
divers check what is close before they cast this one, not after.
