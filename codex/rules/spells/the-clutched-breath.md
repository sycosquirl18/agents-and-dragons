# The Clutched Breath

**Tradition:** [Salvor's marks](../traditions.md#salvors-marks) — a scratched fist, cut into the glass by feel, not sight.
**Load:** 2.
**Effect:** Crush it against your chest on the fifth exchange or later of [held breath](../breath.md). Advantage on
that exchange's Might check against the table in [Held breath](../breath.md).
**Duration:** That one check, immediately.
**Misfire (natural 1):** The mark reads your grip as panic, not need, and answers it: the exchange is called *early*,
one full row up the table, and you roll at **disadvantage** instead of advantage — believing, until the die is
cast, that you were owed the better odds.

Cut for divers past the fourth exchange with no line to the surface and no [Drawn Breath](drawn-breath.md) to
spend. It does not buy air. It only steadies the hand asking for it, and a steadied hand can still be wrong.
