# Last Mark

**Tradition:** Covenant plainscript. Cut in devotional copperplate, one long witnessing-form wrapped around the
edge of the glass. You can read it aloud; you cannot read what it means.

**Load:** 3.

**Effect:** When you cast this, the glass shatters and its shards retain a sensory echo — sight and sound, eight
heartbeats' worth — of the moment it broke. Anyone who holds the shards cupped together may witness that moment
once before the echo fades. After witnessing, the shards go dark and cold. The caster gains advantage on one
Wits check made to recall or describe what they saw immediately after casting.

**Duration:** The echo holds until the shards are first handled, then is gone.

**Misfire (natural 1):** The shards return the wrong moment — not the instant of casting but an eight-heartbeat
echo of the Covenant scriptorium where the glass was inscribed. Unintelligible voices, lamplight, a hand moving
across vellum. Everyone within twenty feet (including the caster) is disoriented: disadvantage on all checks for
one minute. The echo then fades. The glass is spent; you have learned nothing useful, and you have announced
your position to everything that can hear.

**Cost:** Plate only, ~700 silver — if you could buy one. No plainscript spell is in general circulation; the
[Ashen Covenant](../../world/factions/ashen-covenant.md) does not sell. The one known copy is held by the Covenant
Archivist in [Vaultspire](../../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md), who will not confirm its existence.

What the Archivist's copy last witnessed is not known. The Covenant has not permitted it to be handled since year 401.
