# The Steady Hand

**Tradition:** [Salvor's marks](../traditions.md#salvors-marks) — four strokes, and forgers cut their own.
**Load:** 1.
**Effect:** Advantage on a single Grace check, declared before the roll.
**Duration:** One check.
**Misfire (natural 1):** Disadvantage on that check and every Grace check for the next hour. Your hands will not stop
shaking.

Cheap to inscribe and popular with thieves, surgeons, and forgers, which is why possessing one in
[Vaultspire](../../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md) invites a question about what you were about to do.
