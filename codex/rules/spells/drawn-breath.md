# Drawn Breath

**Tradition:** [Salvor's marks](../traditions.md#salvors-marks) — cut cold, on a boat, by someone who needed it.
**Load:** 2.
**Effect:** You breathe water as easily as air. Cold, pressure, and the dark are unaffected — this solves exactly one
of the four things that kill divers.
**Duration:** One hour.
**Misfire (natural 1):** It works, and it does not stop. You breathe water for an hour and *cannot* breathe air for
that hour. Leaving the water before it lapses means suffocating in the open.

The standard salvor's spell, and the reason a Chip of glass costs about what a diver earns in a month. Every crew that
can afford one carries one, and every crew that cannot dives on held breath and a rope.
