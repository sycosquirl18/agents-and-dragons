# The True Edge

**Tradition:** [Kilnworks notation](../traditions.md#kilnworks-notation) — a tolerance gauge, cut for machinists
checking a fit before they trusted their weight to it.

**Load:** 1.

**Effect:** Touch the glass to any single fixed edge, seam, or fitting — a pin, a hinge, a seal, a shackle, a
grate's mounting — and it tells you, without a check, whether that piece is *true*: sound to its original
tolerance, or already flawed and failing. It gives no numbers and no cause, only sound or not. It does not test
people, rope, or anything that was never machined true to begin with.

**Duration:** Instant.

**Misfire (natural 1):** It reads true regardless of what is actually there. A cracked pin, a cut seal, a
grate about to give all come back sound. You have not learned nothing — you have learned something false, and
you will act on it exactly as if you had checked.

Cut for a trade that stopped existing four hundred years ago, and still the only reliable answer to the question
every salvor asks with a hand on a shackle: *will this hold my weight, or has it already stopped being what it
was made to be.* [Brannoc Vell](../../characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) broke three pins on the *Umber Cord* to
find out the hard way what this glass would have told him for the price of a Chip.
