# The Clocked Grip

**Tradition:** [Kilnworks notation](../traditions.md#kilnworks-notation) — the notch cut into the seam counts revs,
like a ratchet pawl that has caught before and expects to catch again.

**Load:** 1 — a quiet failure. Nothing breaks loose but the diver's own hand.

**Effect:** Grip the glass in your working hand, then close that hand on a chain, line, or fitting. For the
duration, that one point of contact cannot be torn loose by force, current, or the weight you are carrying — you
still make every check for footing, balance, or the climb itself; this only holds the grip.

**Duration:** Ten minutes, or until you choose to open your hand.

**Misfire (natural 1):** The catch takes anyway, but takes wrong — it seizes the hand shut on whatever it is
touching, chain, rope, or another diver's wrist, for the full ten minutes, and you cannot open it yourself before
then even to save your own air.

Cut for foundry hands working a winch one-handed, the other arm full or already ruined. Nobody at the Kiln thought
to note that a shut hand does not know the difference between a spool and a person.
