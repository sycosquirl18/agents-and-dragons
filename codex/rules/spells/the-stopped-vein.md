# The Stopped Vein

**Tradition:** [Salvor's marks](../traditions.md#salvors-marks) — scratched straight onto skin at the wound's edge,
in blood if nothing else is to hand. You can tell it is Salvor's because nobody who wasn't bleeding would have
bothered to cut it.
**Load:** 1.
**Effect:** Touch the mark to an open wound. The bleeding stops at once, cleanly, for the duration — no check needed
to avoid bleeding out in that time, though the wound is still there and still hurts.
**Duration:** One hour, or until the wound is properly dressed, whichever comes first.
**Misfire (natural 1):** The clot holds too well. It seals off the vessel entirely rather than just the surface, and
the limb below the wound goes cold, numb, and useless for a full day — a hand that will not close, a foot that will
not bear weight, right when you need it. It does not spread further, and it does not cost you the limb, but for that
day it is not yours to use.

Cut for a diver who is bleeding and has no minutes to spare on it, not for a battlefield medic — it stops the
bleeding, not the fight, and the wound still needs real care before the hour is up.
