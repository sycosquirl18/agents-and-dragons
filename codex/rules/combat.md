# Combat

Combat uses [the same check mechanic](checks.md) as everything else. It is meant to be short and dangerous.

## Order

Everyone rolls `1d20 + Grace`; act in descending order. Roll once at the start, keep it for the fight.

## A turn

Move somewhere reachable, and take **one** action: attack, cast, or do something clever. Doing something clever is
usually better.

## Attacking

`1d20 + Might` (melee, thrown) or `1d20 + Grace` (ranged, finesse) vs the target's **Armour DC**.

| Armour | DC |
| --- | --- |
| None | 10 |
| Padded / leathers | 12 |
| Mail | 14 |
| Kilnworks plate (salvaged) | 16 |

On a hit, roll the weapon's damage and subtract it from HP.

| Weapon | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| Knife, cudgel | 1d4 |
| Sword, axe, spear | 1d6 |
| Two-handed | 1d10 |
| Bow, sling | 1d6 |

**Natural 20:** maximum damage, no roll. **Natural 1:** you miss and lose your position — dropped weapon, off
balance, or exposed.

## Dying

At **0 HP** a hero is **down**: unconscious, bleeding, out of the fight. They die at the end of their next turn unless
someone reaches them (Wits DC 12 to stabilise).

Anyone who dies, dies. There is no revival short of an act of [magic](magic.md) nobody currently knows how to perform.

Creatures at 0 HP are simply finished.

## Recovery

- A short rest and a meal: `1d6` HP, once per day.
- A full night somewhere safe: all HP.
- Nothing heals a hero who has not eaten or slept.

## Running away

Always available, always the right call more often than heroes take it. Fleeing is a contest of Grace against the
fastest pursuer. Losing means one free attack against you; it does not mean you are caught.
