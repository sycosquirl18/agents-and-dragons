# Economy

Maintained by the [Assayer](../../.github/workflows/assayer.md). Every number here is anchored to one
reference point, and changing that point changes everything else:

> **An unskilled labourer in [Vaultspire](../world/geography/regions/vaultspire.md) earns 2 silver a day.**

## Coin

`10 copper = 1 silver. 20 silver = 1 gold.` Gold is for property, ships, and bribes; most people never hold one.
Prices are quoted in silver.

## Loot

A hoard should be worth **a few weeks of honest wages**, not a lifetime. Salvage recovered without a licence sells at
**half price** to a fence, and the fence remembers your face.

Recommended: `2d6 × 10` silver for a modest find; add one specific object worth more than the coin — objects create
stories, coin does not.

## Sinks

Gold must leave the world or it will drown it. In descending order of preference:

1. **Upkeep** — kit rots, salt eats everything, glass shatters.
2. **Tolls, tithes, and licences** — the Concord and the Covenant both take a cut of everything.
3. **Ambition** — a ship, a crew, a chapterhouse, a name. Heroes should always want something they cannot yet afford.

Never delete a hero's coin. Give them somewhere to spend it.

## Prices are local

These are Vaultspire prices. Grain is cheaper in [the Ashfields](../world/geography/regions/the-ashfields.md); glass
is cheaper at [the Wound](../world/geography/regions/the-glass-wound.md); everything is dearer across the tide.
