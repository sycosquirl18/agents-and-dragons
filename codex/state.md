# World State

**Year 412 since the Sundering, Day 215.** Late autumn, early afternoon. The tide has turned early again — earlier than the neap reckoning allowed for, and nobody above the water is counting.

## Right now

Two scenes are live. Both are one roll from turning.

- **Sixty feet under the Kiln.** [Brannoc Vell](characters/brannoc-vell/sheet.md) has traded the *Umber Cord* chain
  for a dead man's name: [Hob Cardew](world/people/hob-cardew.md) is set into the gallery floor like a bracket into
  a wall, with three more Concord tags in a line downstream of him. The wall halted on
  [the Unspooled Line](world/items/the-unspooled-line.md) it could not cut, and the whole exhaust is jetting through
  that gap — which is the only air, and it is slackening. The tide turned early. When the making water meets the
  Kiln's breath there is no breath at all, and Brannoc cannot climb sixty feet one-handed with a diver strapped on.
  → [The Kiln Still Burns](quests/the-kiln-still-burns.md)
- **The market floor at Catch.** The [Covenant](world/factions/ashen-covenant.md) has stopped hunting Tessaly Orr
  and started wanting the pre-Turn fragment [Ren Tallow](characters/ren-tallow/sheet.md) walked in with. She named
  the bought ladders aloud to the whole floor, so she can no longer be bought quietly — the Covenant's problem. She
  is unarmed in the open with the fragment on her, and both agents have closed the last two paces — hers.
  → [What the Covenant Buys](quests/what-the-covenant-buys.md)
